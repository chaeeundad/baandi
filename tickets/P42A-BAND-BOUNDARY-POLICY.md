PHASE B (only if Phase A shows a usable relation): implement `[scf] extra_bands = "auto"` (default stays integer 4, bit-identical) whose rule uses only information that exists before or right after the first SCF iteration without knowing the converged spectrum: candidates are (i) the kinetic/free-electron eigenvalues of the plane-wave basis near the requested boundary (cheap, available at setup), (ii) the eigenvalues of a first loose solve with a few extra buffer bands beyond the candidate boundary, (iii) a fixed small search ladder of candidate counts evaluated on candidate (ii). The rule must be deterministic, thread-count independent, give the same count at all k points (state how), fall back to the integer default when it cannot decide, and report the chosen count in the results metadata. Choose parameters on the training cases only and report the held-out cases untouched. DECISION RULE (write it into the document BEFORE measuring and never relax it): the rule is a recommended option only if, on the held-out cases and perturbations, the median SCF iterations fall or stay equal on at least 80 percent of cases, the median H work does not rise by more than 10 percent on any case and falls on the median over cases, the SCF spread (max - min) of the distribution does not increase on any case by more than 20 percent and shrinks on the median, no case that converges at the default fails, delivered accuracy stays within 1.5 times the default's (strict still certifies), in both the standard and the strict profile, on both paths where applicable. Otherwise report the measured relation and say which condition fails. Never change the default in this ticket.
