Only the order of floating-point summation changes. No Cholesky of a Gram matrix (A52 is not repeated).
  2. b from a shape rule (b such that n x b doubles fit in half of L2, rounded down to a multiple of 4, at least 4), stated in the code; independent of T.

  4. Orthogonality: report max |Q^T Q - I| per call class and the defect gate; report the number of rejected (dependent) columns with on/off on every fixture; any difference in rejected-column counts is a finding to report, not to tune away.
