# MPI in one page

Approved by the user on 2026-11-09 (AGENTS.md scope decision U9). Contract: docs/decisions/0041-mpi-parallel-layer.md; plan and gates: docs/roadmap/mpi-m1-plan.md. Crate `dft-mpi`: FFI for the Open MPI ABI behind feature `mpi` (no MPI on the Mac; type check only), single-rank stand-in without it, `Collective` trait with an in-process simulator, deterministic `tree_sum_f64` (fixed binomial association, bit-identical on every rank for a fixed rank count; faster than allgather plus ordered sum from P = 4, one rule for every P).
Measured (dev08, shared memory, one socket, us per call, P = 2 / 4 / 8): Allgatherv 11877 x 19 columns 82 / 254 / 400; Allreduce 408 x 68 44 / 80 / 129; tree sum 408 x 68 94 / 154 / 228; Alltoall of the correction-block transpose 39 / 37 / 26. Staying inside one socket matters (1.15 to 1.9x on reductions). Multi-node scaling cannot be measured on dev08.
Design: row-block basis with local algebra and tree reductions, H on column pairs with two all-to-all transposes per Davidson step, reduced eigenproblem replicated; Amdahl bound of a band-parallel-only design is about 1.8x at 8 ranks, below the 2.43x of threads, which is why the algebra is distributed too. Gate: si32 strict at least 2.2x at 4 ranks and 3.5x at 8 ranks over one rank.

## Status 2026-11-09 (update)
M1a to M1e are implemented and pass on simulated ranks (Mac, correctness only): `dft_solvers::distributed_davidson` (`RealDavidson::solve_distributed`), `dft_engine::parallel` (thread-local communicator; the engine takes the distributed branch only when the registered communicator has two or more ranks and the preconditioner is the default), and `dft-cli --features mpi`. Non-root ranks write to `<output>/rank<r>/`. Open: dev08 build and smoke run, then the pre-registered scaling gate (ledger row MPI-M1CDE). Non-Davidson stages are replicated per rank in M1.

First dev08 gate (ledger MPI-M1-GATE1): 1.19x at 4 ranks and 1.31x at 8 ranks on si32 strict against the thread path's 2.43x. The extra Davidson iterations (91 to 121) came from Cholesky panel orthonormalisation dropping near-dependent corrections; fixed with a distributed MGS panel (matches serial iteration counts on simulated ranks). Re-measure; the replicated non-Davidson stages are the next Amdahl limit.

Gate re-run (ledger MPI-M1-GATE2): best MPI 1.35x (np=4 x T=2) against threads 2.49x on 8 cores; extra Davidson iterations traced to one cluster-cut empty band at SCF step 10; Mac reproduction suggests sensitivity, not a bug. Results.json now carries per-collective time (`parallel.collectives`).

Ledger MPI-M1-GATE3: perturbed seeds 601-610 are the standard MPI scaling case (unperturbed si32 is anomalously easy); the extra Davidson iterations were sensitivity; communication is at most 13 percent of wall, so the remaining gap is non-scaling compute.

Ledger MPI-M1-GATE4: the distributed Davidson scales only 1.28x at 4 ranks; the replicated stages (24 percent of wall) are the smaller Amdahl term. Reduced matrix is now incremental and per-stage timers are in results.json `parallel.stages`; next measurement attributes the non-BLAS 21.8 s.

Ledger MPI-M1-GATE5: M1 granularity limit found (column pairs divided over ranks with the remainder on the first ranks; late iterations apply few columns). Further scaling needs a distributed FFT (M2). Decision pending with the user: stop at M1 or invest in M2.

Ledger MPI-SCALING-BEYOND-8: M2d reaches parity with threads at np=8 on one socket; beyond 8 ranks the replicated stages double per rank (memory-bandwidth sharing) and np=16 is 60 percent slower, on one socket as on two; the socket boundary and communication are not the cause. MPI work is frozen at M2d until a multi-node resource exists.

Ledger MPI-ENTRY-COST-FIXES (2026-11-14, work order E2): the np=2 penalty of the M2 path is mostly the distributed Davidson algebra (about 70 s excess at si64 strict), not the apply (about 11 s): the classic distributed orthonormalization does about four times the serial work as scalar loops, and distributed rotations, projections and nonlocal products miss dgemm on MKL builds. Opt-in fixes (default off, dev08 A/B pending): `BAANDI_M2_ORTHO=serial`, `BAANDI_M2_DGEMM=on`, `BAANDI_M2_FFT=mkl`, `BAANDI_M2_LEAN=on`; Mac pure si32 np=2 -25 %, np=4 -21 %, identical work. Details: docs/performance/mpi-entry-cost-analysis.md.

Ledger MPI-REDUCED-EIGEN (2026-11-15, work order E2): after the entry-cost fixes the replicated dense reduced eigenproblem is the next ceiling (si64 strict np=8 7.20 of 47.7 s, 15 percent; the same work is 32 percent slower per rank at np=8 than at np=2 because the concurrent replicated solves share the socket). One all-pairs solve per Davidson iteration, orders up to 6 x bands (si64 mean about 460, p90 about 740). Opt-in switches (default off, dev08 A/B pending): `BAANDI_M2_EIGEN=root` (rank 0 solves and broadcasts; bit-identical) and `BAANDI_M2_B5=on` (serial B5 bordered update; rounding class). Smaller capacity does not pay; only a distributed dense EVD (ScaLAPACK/ELPA) would shorten the critical path. Details: docs/performance/mpi-reduced-eigen-analysis.md.

Ledger MPI-COARSE-CERT (2026-11-15, work order E2): at si64 strict np=8 the certificate screen (6.9 s, slower with ranks) and the coarse atomic start (2.7 s, slower with ranks) are replicated single-thread full-grid work on every rank (screen cluster FFTs and products; span, two-pass Gram-Schmidt and complex H Q, because ATOMIC-REAL is threads-only), slowed by concurrent memory traffic, not by collectives. `BAANDI_M2_SCREEN=dist` and `BAANDI_M2_ATOMIC=split` (default off) distribute both (Mac si64 np=8: screen 3.40 -> 0.22 s, atomic start 1.69 -> 0.51 s, wall 40.6 -> 36.3 s, bits unchanged); dev08 A/B pending. The coarse diagonalization (80 of 102 Davidson iterations at si64) is limited by the replicated reduced eigenproblem: M2_EIGEN=root/B5 and a distributed EVD are the levers (docs/performance/mpi-coarse-certificate-analysis.md).

Ledger MPI-FS-* (2026-11-16, fast loop): stage-1 MPI screen harness `scripts/fast_screen_mpi.py` (simulated ranks, counters decide, wall is an observation). On the production default 92 to 94 percent of the solver tree sums are in-panel Gram-Schmidt dot/norm reductions; `BAANDI_M2_PANEL_GS=fused` removes 60 percent of all tree sums with identical work counts (candidate); `BAANDI_MPI_TREE=rd` halves the message rounds bit-identically (dev08 microbenchmark first); `BAANDI_M2_DENSITY=fused` is bit-identical and neutral on the Mac. The verification costs the same share on the serial and MPI paths (failed attempts, not distribution). The M2 path applies H to 29 percent more columns than the serial path (no kept start): next structural item. Profile: docs/performance/profile-2026-11-16-mpi.md.

Ledger MPI-KEPT-START (2026-11-16): `BAANDI_M2_REUSE=on` (opt-in, rounding class) brings the C2 kept start to the stick rows: local X and H X kept per rank, plane slabs of the occupied pairs kept by the M2 density build, next start H_new X = H_old X + (V_new - V_old) X without orthonormalization or initial apply (replicated decision: initial = X to 1e-14, Gram defect <= 1e-12). Mac counters: H columns -21 % (si32) / -27 % (si64), inverse pair FFTs -18 / -20 %, tree sums -24 / -27 %, SCF and Davidson counts identical. The return allgatherv stays (the engine still consumes complex full orbitals). Design docs/design/mpi-kept-start.md; dev08 stage 2 docs/performance/requests/mpi-kept-start.md.

Ledger MPI-SCALING-GAP-* (2026-11-17): why the M2 solve speeds up only 2.2x from np 2 to 8 on dev08. Amdahl on the dev08 cold solve: about 61 ms does not shrink (56 percent of the np=8 solve). A split into cost per H column and per Davidson iteration puts about 0.3 ms per H column (the distributed apply: two FFT transposes plus two mirror exchanges per apply whose node volume is independent of np, plus in-panel reductions) and about 1.7 ms per iteration (replicated reduced eigenproblem) outside the parallel part. New opt-in switches: `BAANDI_M2_STICKS=mirror` (stick and mirror stick on one rank: no mirror alltoalls, alltoall calls -50 percent, bytes -28 to -34 percent) and `BAANDI_M2_START=gram` (orthonormal starts taken after one Gram reduction: tree sums -29 percent); Mac SCF si32 np=8 -8.4 percent together (observation). Capacity 2 rejected. Next levers: distributed full-grid FFT for the replicated Hartree/audit/mixing, lazy orbitals (no N x B gather), distributed back-transform of the reduced eigenproblem. Analysis docs/performance/mpi-scaling-gap.md; dev08 request docs/performance/requests/mpi-scaling-gap.md (Part A measures the per-stage seconds and the alltoall/allgatherv time per call at np 2/4/8).

Ledger MPI-LAZY-ORBITALS-* (2026-11-17): the dev08 microbenchmark showed that the N x B allgatherv at every M2 solve return grows with np (si32 557 -> 2050 us per call from np 2 to 8, si64 4551 -> 9994 us) while the transposes scale. `BAANDI_M2_LAZY=on` (opt-in) keeps the eigenvectors as each rank's stick rows inside the SCF: the solve returns local rows only, the next solve starts from them, the audit takes the kinetic energy from them (one small reduction), and the full complex block is gathered once at the end (also at a ladder hand-off, checkpoint or explicit audit). The SCF trajectory is bit-identical to the switch off (density, eigenvalues, orbitals, counts); only the reported kinetic energy is summed in another order (1e-14 Ha). Mac counters: allgatherv volume -54 percent (si32) and -65 percent (si64) per run, no complex unpack per solve. Consumer inventory and design docs/design/mpi-lazy-orbitals.md; dev08 request docs/performance/requests/mpi-lazy-orbitals.md.

Ledger MPI-REMAINING-* (2026-11-17): with the new defaults (lazy orbitals, fused panel Gram-Schmidt, verify half, selected roots, rd tree) the stages that do not shrink from np 4 to 8 are the panel Gram-Schmidt chain, the replicated reduced eigenproblem and the waits after the rotation inside the solve (about 58 percent of the np=8 warm si32 solve on the Mac), and outside it the ladder coarse stage (a quarter of the np=8 wall), the verification and the replicated full-grid stages (potential, audit, mixing, stop rule; about 9 percent and growing). At one core the same Davidson-algebra stages are the furthest from QE; QE never Gram-Schmidts the expanded basis (overlap matrix plus generalized reduced problem), so the next lever is an overlap-matrix Davidson on the M2 path (backlog). New opt-in switches: `BAANDI_MIX_INCREMENTAL=on` (Pulay keeps its differences and Gram entries: bit-identical, mixing phase about -48 percent) and `BAANDI_STOP_METRIC=kept` (stop-rule estimate from the Fourier coefficients the Hartree potential and the audit already computed: one full-grid FFT pair per map removed, rounding class of the estimate). The pending solver switches (mirror sticks, Gram start, kept start) were tested together with lazy orbitals for the first time: standard class held, Mac SCF -7 to -12 percent. Nonblocking alltoall rejected before code (the transposes scale on dev08). Analysis docs/performance/mpi-remaining.md; dev08 request docs/performance/requests/mpi-remaining.md (also asks for QE's MPI stage timers).

Ledger MPI-OVERLAP-DAVIDSON-* (2026-11-17): `BAANDI_M2_LEAN_ALGEBRA=on` (opt-in, rounding class) brings the serial lean solver's overlap-matrix algebra to the M2 stick rows on standard solves: corrections are appended without Gram-Schmidt, the overlap S and its inverse Cholesky factor are kept beside the reduced Hamiltonian (replicated, small), the reduced problem is generalized, and the pivot test with the serial fallbacks guards dependence (same rank decisions as the serial lean solver, tested on 1 to 4 ranks). Two tree sums per Davidson iteration instead of about 4 + k; the pivot test must run before H (a one-sum variant applied H to rejected blocks: rejected). Mac screens: warm solve -12 to -25 percent, production SCF -6 to -10 percent, tree sums -66 percent (-89 percent with the Gram start), H columns and iteration counts unchanged. Ledger RESTART-KEEP-* (2026-11-17, BREAKTHROUGH-1-RESTART): keeping previous search directions at a lean contraction restart (`BAANDI_RESTART_KEEP`, GD+k, no extra H application, no extra reduction) is REJECT at the MPI capacity rule (3 per band): an si32 np=8 production SCF has only 11 contractions in 22 solves, the kept columns take room from the next correction block and H columns do not fall (-0.6 to +0.6 %) while rotation FLOPs rise. Ledger INIT-* (2026-11-18, INIT-SCALE): dev08 showed `init` flat from np 2 to 8 (si32 0.63 s, 12.7 to 21.5 percent of the 10-step run; si64 atomic orthonormalize 0.849 s). Not done: the complex nonlocal table in `states` (memory-bound, needs owned-row construction, ISSUE-20 part 3).

Ledger INIT-SCALE-2 (2026-11-18): the init parts that still grew from np 2 to 8 were replicated per rank.
