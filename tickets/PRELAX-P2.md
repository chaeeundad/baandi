TASK_ID: PRELAX-P2
GOAL: Native Rust preconditioned ionic optimizer (opt-in, not the default), following the PRELAX-P1 evidence on dev08 (2026-11-27/28): a bond-spring model-Hessian preconditioned L-BFGS ("A3") needed a median 0.38-0.41x the DFT evaluations of native BFGS on Si8/Si8-AlP and 0.22x on the 63-atom Si-Al-P vacancy cases (ASE PreconLBFGS Exp 0.39-0.50x, ASE LBFGS 0.85-0.88x), with no extra failures and no different minima. Pre-registered Phase-2 rule (precon-relax-design.md): >= 20 % fewer median evaluations than both native BFGS and ASE LBFGS — met by a wide margin.

SCOPE:
1. `[relax] method = "precon_lbfgs"` (name final in the ADR) in ions mode (ions_cell later, out of scope here): L-BFGS with a physical preconditioner H0 = P^-1, P a sparse SPD model Hessian on the free Cartesian coordinates built from neighbour springs (covalent/ionic radii neighbour list under PBC, smooth exponential stiffness in d/r0, stretch-dominant 3x3 blocks, plus a small isotropic stabiliser), fixed atoms eliminated, translations handled (the stabiliser keeps P SPD).
2. Generality: no system-tuned constants. The overall scale mu of P must be estimated (e.g. from the first secant pair(s) or a Rayleigh-quotient fit to observed curvature, without an extra DFT evaluation); the stiffness shape parameters are documented defaults with a reasoned physical basis, validated on the dev08 benchmark set and at least one structure class outside Si-Al-P (an ionic oxide and a molecule/surface case) before any default discussion.
3. Rebuild P when atoms move more than a fraction of the neighbour distance (document the rule); keep L-BFGS pairs across rebuilds or reset them, measured.
4. Line search: Armijo with noise tolerance tied to the SCF energy accuracy (ADR 0057 gives the threshold), max step cap, fallback to steepest descent in the P metric and reset on non-descent; failed-SCF retry semantics as for BFGS.
5. Sparse linear algebra: faer sparse Cholesky (already adopted crate) or block CG; no new dependency without docs/dependencies.md.
6. Restart/checkpoint: optimizer state (pairs, mu, P build geometry) in restart.json like BFGS; deterministic.
7. Outputs: same frames/relax.log fields as BFGS plus mu, P rebuild events and line-search trial counts; every attempted evaluation counted.
8. Tests (spec section 12): SPD of P for random cells incl. fixed atoms and PBC images; exact convergence on a quadratic model where P equals the true Hessian (1-2 iterations); invariance to rigid translation/permutation; restart bit-identity; a small DFT relaxation (debug-fast or #[ignore] with release command) converging with fewer evaluations than BFGS.
9. ADR (next free number) and docs/user/relaxation.md section; the harness under tests/reference/relax-precon gains a native arm so dev08 can rerun stages 1-3 with it.
