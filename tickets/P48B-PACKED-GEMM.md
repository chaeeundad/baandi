TASK_ID: P48B-PACKED-GEMM
GOAL: Make the pure-Rust real GEMM sites run as packed, cache-blocked BLAS-3 instead of faer nano-GEMM calls on 16x16x16 and 4x4x256 sub-blocks (`dft-numerics/src/real.rs::bounded_matmul`, used by `gram`, `rotate_legacy`, `multiply_into`, `multiply_slices_*`), so that the pure build reaches at least 60 percent of MKL dgemm GFLOP/s at the Davidson and nonlocal shapes, with results independent of the thread count. Rounding class. Accuracy contract: docs/performance/requests/p48-work-order.md section 2.
OWNED_FILES: crates/dft-numerics/src/real.rs (GEMM kernels and workspaces), crates/dft-numerics/src/linalg/tiled.rs (only if the complex workspace shares the kernel; complex sites otherwise belong to P48C), crates/dft-numerics/src/switches.rs (one new switch `BAANDI_PURE_GEMM=packed|nano`, default nano until adopted), crates/dft-numerics/Cargo.toml only if `gemm` or `pulp` is used directly (both are already in Cargo.lock through faer; add the entry to docs/dependencies.md), crates/dft-numerics/benches or tests (new microbench and identity tests), docs/performance/optimization-log.md, attempts-ledger.md, backlog.md.
READ_ONLY_CONTEXT: AGENTS.md (Library policy ADR 0003: prefer maintained crates; hot-path rules); docs/performance/AGENT-BRIEF.md; ledger rows A49 (finer GEMM subtiles, rejected), ROT1 (auto-vectorised blocked kernel, rejected), A55, P14B; stage-decomposition-results.md section 3 point 3; the comment above `bounded_matmul` (why nano tiles were chosen: faer's general GEMM allocates opaque packing buffers).
INPUT_CONTRACT: P48A per-site shapes and GFLOP/s (or, until it lands, the 11-09 decomposition: rotation 17 GFLOP/s pure vs 85 MKL). Shapes are tall-skinny: n (half-sphere plane waves, about 1.5e3 to 2.5e4) by m (basis, up to 6 x bands) times m by k (k from 1 to bands).
PHYSICS_CONVENTIONS: none touched.
IMPLEMENTATION_STEPS:
  1. Establish whether a maintained kernel can run packed GEMM without allocation in the steady state: (a) faer `matmul` / `gemm` crate with `Par::Seq` on full panels, measured with a counting global allocator in a test: allocations after the first call of each shape class must be zero (thread-local packing caches that grow once are acceptable if the test proves no growth after warm-up on the benchmark shape set); (b) if (a) allocates in the steady state, a packed micro-kernel written with `pulp` (already in the tree; safe SIMD dispatch) with packing buffers owned by `RealGemmWorkspace` and sized at construction from (long, short) capacity. Prefer (a); record the evidence for the choice.
  2. Keep determinism by construction: the decomposition into row panels over the reduction (Gram, PANEL = 256 today) and into column groups (rotation) depends only on the shapes, never on the worker count; partial sums are combined in ascending panel order (as `assemble` does now); each panel product is computed by one worker with `Par::Seq`. Choose panel sizes by a cache rule written in the code comment (for example panel rows such that the A and B panels fit in L2 of the target, with the rule stated, not a per-machine constant tuned on one CPU); document that results are identical across T on one machine and may differ in the last bits across CPU families (as MKL does).
  3. Route every real GEMM site through the new kernel behind `BAANDI_PURE_GEMM` (MKL builds unchanged): projection overlap and subtract, reduced matrix, rotation pair, Ritz/residual, nonlocal P^T X and back product in `real_gamma.rs` (call sites only; the nonlocal file belongs to dft-physics, so request a one-line change there through the orchestrator or extend OWNED_FILES), B1 sketch products.
  4. Microbench per site shape (from P48A) on the Mac and as a dev08 request: GFLOP/s nano vs packed vs MKL, T=1 and T=8.
  5. End-to-end: generality set of the work order, pure native build, paired same-binary A/B (`BAANDI_PURE_GEMM=nano` vs `packed`), seeds 601-610.
TEST_COMMANDS: scripts/check.sh; cargo test -p dft-numerics (new identity tests: packed vs nano within 4 ulp per entry on random and ill-conditioned inputs; bit-identical across T=1,2,4,8 for packed; counting-allocator steady-state test); python3 -I scripts/bench_scf.py --require-identical on the protocol cases with the switch off (bit-identical) and the energy table with it on; scripts/bench_suite.py --robustness.
ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA: accuracy contract of the work order (rounding class); zero steady-state allocations proven by test; density SHA identical across T=1,2,4,8 with packed; per-site GFLOP/s at least 60 percent of MKL at T=1 on dev08 for the three largest sites at si32 (E target; adoption needs only a median wall gain per the adoption rule); median wall improves on dev08 pure native at si32 with no regression beyond noise elsewhere.
NON_GOALS: MKL path changes; complex k-path nonlocal (P48C); algorithm changes in Davidson; GPU.
DEPENDENCIES: P48A for shapes (soft). P48D builds on this kernel for the pure build.
