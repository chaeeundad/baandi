TASK_ID: P31A-DAVIDSON-CAPACITY
GOAL: Test whether the Davidson subspace capacity (hard-wired to at least 6 x bands in every solve path) explains the growth of BAANDI's gap to QE with system size, and whether a smaller capacity is a robust speedup. Evidence (tests/reference/perf/dev08_materials_qe_relayed.md): at equal delivered accuracy and one core the gap to QE real gamma grows 6.8x, 8.3x, 17.8x for si8, si16, si32 (strict); per H vector BAANDI costs 1.84 ms on si8 and 20 ms on si32 (x11 for x4.6 expected from N log N FFT plus x3.4 bands), which points to the dense subspace algebra (cost about N x capacity^2 with capacity = 6 x 68 = 408 for si32). QE's block capacity is 2 x bands (docs/research/qe-study/qe-solvers-and-subspace.md and qe-solvers notes, own words; backlog B17; ledger rows A08/A13 tested capacity only at 8 bands).
