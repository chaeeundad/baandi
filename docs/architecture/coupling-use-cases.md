# Coupling use cases A to G: capability matrix (design; nothing implemented)

Status: reconciled with [coupling-codex-review.md](../research/coupling-codex-review.md) section 7. DFT-CES and DFT-CES2 are examples of external drivers, not dependencies. "Feasible today" means the present engine: neutral, even-electron, spin-unpolarized, insulating, norm-conserving LDA-PZ, Bloch k, no forces, no stress.
| Case | Data exchanged | Required core capability | External responsibilities and physical constraints | Present state and gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Fixed electrostatic embedding | electron potential energy grid on the engine grid plus cell, origin, reference and environment id in; density, energy ledger, eigenpairs and residuals out | scalar field injection, an audited `integral n U` energy term, optional electrostatic outputs | build a compatible periodic field; keep electron-only perturbation apart from ion-inclusive electrostatics; state the gauge and neutrality | operator primitives and density export exist; SCF injection and the ledger do not. First bounded extension, not usable today |
| B. Mean-field explicit solvent (DFT-CES family) | averaged solvent charge or potential, or model-specific terms in; density and qualified electrostatic observables out | A plus validated definitions of electrostatic sources and outputs; richer terms only through supported operators or functionals | solvent sampling or MD, averaging, thermodynamic functional, outer mixing and convergence, environment free energy | QM snapshots possible; no coupled loop, no source reconstruction. An average of fields is not an average free energy; not every CES term reduces to a scalar potential |
| C. Polarizable or density-dependent QM/MM | permanent charges, induced response and parameters, potential in; density and possibly reaction forces out | functional-derivative-consistent embedding; forces only after a validated derivative implementation | polarization solver, self energy, exclusions, link and boundary treatment, force partition, joint stationarity | none of the coupled workflow exists; fixed-field snapshots could follow A; no conservative dynamics or force training |
| D. Implicit solvation | reaction field, dielectric and cavity state, functional components in; density and cavity response out | a functional derivative with the matching energy; cavity and geometry derivatives later | continuum PDE solver, solvent free energy, non-electrostatic terms, boundary matching | one-way analysis of an exported density possible; no self-consistent solvent. The linear-response one-half rule cannot be assumed for density-dependent cavities |
| E. MLIP and active learning | structures, energy ledger, state and environment ids, quality in; requested forces and stress | qualified observables; later validated derivatives | sampling, labeling, training and inference, uncertainty selection, provenance | energy-only datasets possible now; force and stress columns are unavailable, not zero; branches and environments stay distinct; state ids are still a proposal |
| F. FEM parameter export | microscopic cell, energy and property data with orientation, units and validity domain | energy scans now; stress and elastic response only after validated derivatives | strain sampling, homogenization, temperature, microstructure, constitutive fitting, boundary conditions | an external energy-versus-strain study can be designed; no built-in stress or elastic tensor; a fitted 0 K tensor needs basis and k convergence and is not a finite-temperature law |
| G. ESLE under an environment | initial state or branch hint and environment in; evaluated states, lineage, qualified energies out | A plus continuation and the state archive (ESLE phase B) | multi-start policy, branch hypotheses, common-environment comparison, total free-energy ranking | seeded reruns possible but the SCF starts from a uniform density; electronic metastability for the present insulating Si is not established |
Common rules: coupled states are ranked only within one environment or by a complete free energy; a missing derivative is reported unavailable; MLIP and FEM exports preserve state labels, cutoff, k sampling, convergence, pseudopotential hashes and the energy-accounting scope.
