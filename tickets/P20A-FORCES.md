TASK_ID: P20A-FORCES
GOAL: Master plan MF12 + MF13: forces on atoms for norm-conserving plane-wave calculations, following the APPROVED contracts ADR 0033 (grids, derivative ledger, units: force in Ha/Bohr, stress later), ADR 0031 (spin), ADR 0032 (occupations: forces are derivatives of the variational free energy F), docs/architecture/state-contracts.md, docs/validation/stage-oracles.md rows MF12 and MF13. Forces are required for relaxation (MF15) and for any dynamics; "unavailable" must never be reported as zero.
SCOPE: (1) Derivative ledger (ADR 0033): every energy term has a named owner and derivative capability; the force query returns the sum of ALL active terms or fails with the list of terms lacking a derivative (never a partial total): (a) Ewald (ion-ion) force, (b) local pseudopotential force (structure-factor derivative acting on the valence density in G space), (c) nonlocal projector force (Kleinman-Bylander derivative: for every atom, channel and band with occupations, Hermitian structure; include multiple projectors per l and the D coupling matrix), (d) nonlinear core correction force (derivative of the core-charge structure factor against V_xc, P13C), (e) XC for LDA, lda-pw and PBE enters only through the density and the core term (no separate term except through NLCC), (f) finite-temperature occupations: forces of F with the occupations of the converged state (no extra term when the density is self-consistent; document), (g) spin-polarized collinear channels (sum over channels), (h) symmetry: when `[symmetry] mode = "auto"` is used, symmetrize the forces with the same operations (P19A) and report the symmetrized result; with symmetry off the plain sum. (2) Preconditions: a force request requires the strict convergence profile and a passing fixed-point certificate; `standard` profile results reject force requests with an ADR 0022 style message; unconverged states reject. (3) Interfaces: engine API `forces(&state)` returning Cartesian forces per atom in Ha/Bohr plus the per-term breakdown (Ewald, local, nonlocal, core), the net force (sum), and the maximum absolute component; CLI `[output] forces = true` writing the results.json `forces` block (the proposal schema docs/schemas/results-state.proposal.json: status available, units, per-term arrays; unavailable terms listed) and a text table; ASE calculator in python/baandi: `get_forces` returns the values for supported cases instead of NotImplementedError (keep the NotImplementedError for unsupported combinations such as stress or Hubbard). (4) Oracles (stage-oracles MF13): central finite differences of the CONVERGED total energy (or free energy for finite T) with respect to each Cartesian displacement of each atom, step sizes h = 0.01, 0.005, 0.0025 Bohr, three-point O(h^2) trend, SCF and cutoff tightened (the derivative of a fixed-basis calculation: displacements keep the plane-wave set fixed, cutoff sphere fixed by the reciprocal lattice): synthetic cases first: (i) 2-atom cubic cell with synthetic local-only pseudopotential (analytic structure), (ii) synthetic nonlocal with two projectors per l, (iii) synthetic NLCC dataset, (iv) distorted diamond-like Si with the PseudoDojo files (ignored test, DFT_NC_DIR) and distorted MgO (a small displacement of O), (v) a unpolarized spin-polarized pair, (vi) Fermi-Dirac synthetic metal (free energy derivative), (vii) skewed cell. Caps: synthetic 1e-6 Ha/Bohr, net force below 1e-8 Ha/Bohr at a resolved grid (translation invariance), real-file caps 1e-4 Ha/Bohr only if the uncertainty (SCF, cutoff, FFT grid) is below one third of the cap, else report inconclusive and refine inputs; never enlarge a cap to fit a result. Term-isolated checks: Ewald alone against its own finite difference; local alone with the density frozen; nonlocal alone with frozen orbitals (Hellmann-Feynman partial derivatives), so that an error is attributed to a term. (5) Independent check: an independent brute-force force from the energy functional of a two-atom molecule-like cell in a test-side reimplementation of the Ewald and local energies. (6) Cost: forces cost one extra pass over the bands for the nonlocal term; report the time share on si8 and k444s (Mac observation; counters).
