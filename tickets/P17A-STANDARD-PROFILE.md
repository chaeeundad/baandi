TASK 1: add `[scf] convergence_profile = "strict" | "standard"` (default strict). Under `standard`: (a) SCF stopping uses a Hartree-weighted error estimate (energy of the density residual in the Coulomb metric, divided by nothing: documented in Hartree, with a configurable threshold `standard_threshold_ha`, default chosen by experiment E1 below and then frozen), (b) the in-loop eigensolver tolerance follows the ratcheted schedule with the first-iteration redo safeguard (caps and floors documented in Hartree, written in BAANDI's own terms), (c) the eigensolver stops a root by the Ritz-value change rule; buffer and empty roots use a looser threshold; occupied roots and the first empty root are never allowed to stay unconverged (documented difference from QE, which may accept a few unconverged roots), (d) no fixed-point certificate solve; results carry `convergence_profile: "standard"`, `certified: false`, the thresholds and the work counters, and the CLI and checkpoint provenance print the profile; (e) Hartree-weighted Pulay metric as an independent switch `[scf] mixing_metric = "l2" | "hartree"` (default l2, experiment E4 of qe-scf-cycle.md) so that its effect can be measured separately from the profile. A standard result is never reported as certified and is rejected as input for validation claims by the validation scripts (add the check to scripts/verify_qe.py and compare_materials_qe.py).
