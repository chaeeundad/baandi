CORRECTION of my earlier statement ("delaying the first attempt by one map does not save time"): the maps run between the failed attempt and the passing attempt are NEEDED ANYWAY (np8: iteration 10 -> 11 is one map; one core: iteration 11 -> 13 is two maps).
