## 1. Where the gap is (one core, then eight)

QE real Gamma path, equal delivered-accuracy class, BAANDI best setting (relayed batch-7 D, M): si8 / si16 / si32 standard 2.80 / 3.75 / 4.40, strict class 5.37 / 6.09 / 14.51. Eight cores (D, QE np=8 valid only for si16, si32): 5.6 and 8.8 (standard). QE scales 2.5 and 3.0 from 1 to 8 ranks, BAANDI 1.7 and 1.5 (best-of). Per stage, si8, one core (D, section 0 of the stage note): QE spends 49 percent in h_psi (0.22 ms per H vector, about 800 vectors), 11 percent in Davidson algebra, 11 percent in setup, 21 percent in density, potential and mixing; BAANDI strict spends 32 percent local FFT (0.54 ms per H vector including nonlocal, 2.4 times QE), 3 percent nonlocal, 48 percent algebra (about 30 times QE's absolute time), 16 percent other. H-vector counts are no longer the gap on the unperturbed standard trajectory (772 to 864 against about 800) but are 1.4 times QE on the median perturbed trajectory (P29A, M). SCF iterations: QE 12 / 10 (si8 / si16), BAANDI 15 strict, standard median 24.5 / 20.

The five largest stage-level differences, in order of wall impact (D, E): (1) eigensolver structure (non-orthonormal generalized basis, unconverged-only work); (2) cost per H vector, i.e. the FFT kernel of the pair path on x86; (3) SCF trajectory: atomic start, Hartree-metric history, ratchet and weak stopping rule; (4) decomposition over the plane-wave index (QE) against band/pair parallelism (BAANDI); (5) per-iteration bookkeeping and ionic-step restarts (energy by double counting against a full audit, G-space star symmetrisation, orbital and density carry-over, analytic stress without cutoff scans).

## 3. Ledger check (never repeat without new evidence)

- **A13, P31A, A08 (capacity):** not repeated. The change in premise is structural: capacity 2 failed because each BAANDI step is expensive and orthonormal, so restarts waste memory. Row 4.4 is tested only after 4.1 cuts the per-step cost (P31A text: "dense-algebra improvements that preserve the useful larger subspace remain a separate premise").
- **A16, B13, A52 (generalized basis, CholeskyQR2):** A16 gated; B13 reopen condition (backlog: algebra above 40 percent of wall on a dev08 profile after native and standard) is met for the real strict profile by D0 (48.3 percent at T=1, 57.1 percent at T=8), not yet shown for standard. New evidence stated here: (a) D0 after P32A/P33A, (b) QE gets its cost from this structure with algebra at 11 to 18 percent. A52's skew regression (233 to 329 iterations) is the mandatory stage-0 diagnostic: rank and smallest Gram eigenvalue log on the skewed fixture before any solver change. The generalized solver makes its rank decision in the small problem, not in vector space; this is not CholeskyQR2.
- **P24A (locking, A56):** not repeated. P24A kept the orthonormal all-root structure and removed only prefix roots (-6.0 and -12.5 percent MACs, H +21 to 24 percent). The new item removes the all-root products structurally and without hard locking; the stage-0 and gates below use P24A's failed cases (syn-s2, Si8 buffers, skew) as required.
- **A14 (fuller preconditioner):** not repeated (row 4.7); QE's richer diagonal belongs to a different solver structure but A14's "do not retry without a case where kinetic fails" stands.
- **A15, A21, P29A-2, P29B-2/3 (stopping and tolerance factors, stall guards):** not repeated. Q3 does not change tolerance factors; it changes start and mixer metric and runs the P29A perturbation distribution, which P17A-5 (single trajectories) never did. This is the premise change.
- **A28 (band-grouped FFT), A11/A34 (lane staging, packed-lane threads):** not repeated; QE does not group bands on CPU either. B24 (narrow-block FFT split) revives A34 on a persistent pool with the new evidence in thread-width-analysis.md and needs dev08 wake data first.
- **A33, A35 (scoped OS threads):** not repeated; every item uses the persistent pool.
- **A43, A58b (nonlocal traversal, band-blocked nonlocal):** closed; nonlocal is 3 percent of the real path and parity with QE; B22(a) only hides its time behind the local FFT without changing traversal.
- **P29A/P29B (standard profile robustness):** the standard SCF median (24.5 against 12) is the open problem Q3 attacks; any standard-profile claim must use the perturbed distribution, not the single 15-iteration trajectory.
- **A22 (RMM), B11:** blocked; QE's RMM is a single sweep and uncertifiable.
- **A55 (flops do not map to time on pure Rust):** why Q2 is gated on a dev08 profile and a pure-native timing, not on a MAC count.

## 4. Ranking by expected gain over risk

1. Q1 measurement package (no code, decides rows 3.1, 4.1, 6.1, 6.2): certain value, no risk.
2. Q2 QE-structured real Davidson (row 4.1 to 4.5): largest E gain (wall factor 0.6 to 0.8 at T=1), highest risk; isolated module, default off, stage-0 diagnostic first.
3. Q3 start and mixer package (rows 1.3, 1.4, 2.1, 2.4, 5.2): E 7 to 20 percent of strict work, larger on standard medians; low risk; fixed point unchanged.
4. Q4 persistent real-path state (3.3, optionally 5.1, 5.3): E 3 to 6 percent, bit-identical, very low risk; can run beside Q3.
5. Q5 pair FFT parity (3.1, 3.4): E 10 to 25 percent, medium risk; the only route past QE's per-band FFT time (MKL pruned stick pipeline); needs Q1 first.
6. Q6 overlap of independent phases, cheap non-final energy, narrow FFT split (2.3, 6.1, 6.3): E 10 to 20 percent at T=8, 4 percent at T=1; bit-identical except the energy history; one decision (history).
7. Q7 k-point layout (6.2): E 5 to 15 percent on k meshes at T >= 4, bit-identical.
8. Q8 force, stress and relaxation package (7.1 to 7.3): relaxation and stress workloads only.

Items with no action: 1.1, 1.2, 1.5 (reference only), 2.2, 3.2, 3.5, 4.6 to 4.8.

## 6. Honest end state (E, wide ranges)

si8 real Gamma, one core, strict class: today 2.96 s (M, main b022b61, real on, strict, T=1) against QE conv_thr 1e-10 at 0.551 s (5.4). Factors (E, not independent): Q2 0.68 to 0.78, Q3 0.87 to 0.93, Q5 0.85 to 0.93, Q4 and Q6 together 0.93 to 0.97: product 0.47 to 0.65, i.e. 1.4 to 1.9 s and a ratio of 2.5 to 3.5 to QE. Standard profile: 1.30 s today (unperturbed trajectory) against 0.467 s; with the same factors about 0.8 to 1.0 s, ratio 1.7 to 2.1, and the median trajectory is 1.4 times worse until Q3 shows a better distribution. Parity at one core on Gamma is therefore not reached by these items alone; exceeding needs both a QE-class per-band FFT (Q5 with the pruned MKL pipeline is the only item that can go below QE-MKL) and a scaling model that uses the plane-wave index (Q6, B24). At eight cores the gap (5.6 to 8.8 today) shrinks only with Q2 (serial MGS and EVD disappear or shrink), Q4 and Q6. k-point meshes: QE symmetric k444s 0.805 s against BAANDI auto 1.70 s at equal grid (2.1, M); Q7 and Q2 (complex path later) are the levers, and QE's `-nk` reference is missing.

What would change this plan: Q1 showing that the pair FFT per call is already near MKL (then Q5 drops and Q2 carries everything); the stage-0 diagnostic showing rank loss on the skewed fixture (then Q2 keeps orthonormalisation for restarts only); the standard-profile D0 showing algebra below 40 percent (then Q2 is deferred in favour of Q3, Q5).
