# MPI M2: G-vector distributed FFT and distributed SCF stages (plan, not implemented)

Status 2026-11-10. Approved by the user as scope decision U10 (AGENTS.md); builds on ADR 0041 and `docs/roadmap/mpi-m1-plan.md`. Evidence that motivates it: ledger rows MPI-M1-GATE5 and MPI-M1-CEILING.

## Why M1 stops scaling
M1 divides the H application over column pairs. On si32 strict 66 percent of apply calls carry one column, so at most one rank works in those calls; a perfectly balanced pair split cannot exceed 3.9x at 8 ranks. The replicated stages (potential, density, total_energy, mixing) are 7.4 s of 41 s at one rank and grow with the rank count (10.3 s at 8 ranks). Only splitting the work of ONE column, i.e. the 3D FFT, removes both limits.

## Design
- Row partition of the ORC basis by FFT sticks (columns of G vectors along z), balanced by stick length. The M1 algebra (`distributed::{gram, rotate_local, project_out, ...}`, the MGS panel, the reduced matrix) is independent of how rows are partitioned, so it is reused unchanged.
- Distributed paired real FFT: pack the pair, 1D transforms along z on the local sticks, one all-to-all transpose to xy planes owned by plane, 2D transforms on planes, local multiplication by the potential on the owned planes, and the inverse path. The transpose uses `Collective::alltoall_f64` (already in the trait).
- Nonlocal operator: projector columns are row-partitioned like the basis; P^T x is a local GEMM plus the deterministic tree sum of a (projectors x columns) matrix, the coupling is replicated, P W is local.
- Kinetic term is diagonal and local.
- Replicated stages: density accumulation uses the same distributed transforms (planes reduced into the owner), potential build and total_energy work on the plane slabs with scalar reductions; mixing keeps the density in the plane distribution (the Pulay history is distributed, dot products by tree sum).
- Determinism contract (ADR 0041): one rank reproduces the threads-only path bit for bit (the distributed code is only taken when ranks >= 2); a fixed rank count is bit-reproducible; different rank counts agree within the accuracy classes.

## Stages and gates (fixed before coding)
- M2a: stick partition and distributed paired FFT with simulated ranks. Gate: forward then inverse reproduces the input to 4 n eps relative, equals the serial transform within 8 n eps relative for 1..8 simulated ranks, bit-reproducible at fixed rank count.
- M2b: distributed H application (kinetic, local potential, nonlocal). Gate: H applied to random pairs equals the serial `RealHamiltonian::apply_block` within 1e-12 relative for 1..8 simulated ranks, on the existing synthetic pseudopotential fixtures.
- M2c: Davidson on the stick partition (reuse of the M1 solver with the distributed operator). Gate: eigenvalues within the accuracy classes of the serial solver on the existing Davidson tests and the clustered fixtures; total Davidson work (recorded plus certificate) in the perturbed-start distribution of the serial solver.
- M2d: distributed density, potential, energy and mixing. Gate: SCF total energy within 5e-9 Ha (standard) and 1e-10 Ha (strict) of the threads-only run on the engine tests, all converged, density SHA reproducible at fixed rank count.
- M2e: dev08 scaling gate through the master session, perturbed seeds 601-610, MKL build, load-gated, np=1 of the same binary as baseline, with the proof that the distributed branch ran (rank directories, nonzero dE at 1e-13, `parallel.collectives` call counts). Pass requires all of: (1) np=4 speedup >= 2.2x and np=8 >= 3.5x over np=1 (median over seeds) on si32 strict; (2) at least one MPI configuration on the same 8 physical cores is as fast as or faster than the threads-only path (np=1 x T=8); (3) communication seconds (`parallel.collectives`) below 25 percent of the wall at np=8; (4) accuracy and determinism as in M2d. If (2) fails the result is recorded as "MPI does not beat threads on one node" and M2 is not extended further without a multi-node measurement.

## Risks and open points
- dev08 is one node (shared memory and TCP only): the scaling gate measures the algorithm, not network behaviour.
- The paired real FFT pairs two columns per transform; the distributed version keeps pairing (a single-column call still pays a pair), unless the second-vector work (docs/roadmap/second-vector-plan.md) changes the call mix.
- The plan fixes interfaces only; the grid slab partition, the stick balancing rule and the FFT backend per rank (MKL DFTI, rustfft) are decided in M2a with measurements on the transform sizes of si16 and si32.

## Status
- M2a implemented (2026-11-10): `crates/dft-solvers/src/distributed_fft.rs` (`DistributedPairFft`); gate tests pass with simulated ranks 1..8 (G->r field vs serial FftPlan3d worst rel 3.1e-16; apply_local bit-identical to PairFusedPlan for all rank counts tested; round trip <= 6.7e-16).
- M2b implemented (2026-11-10): `crates/dft-physics/src/distributed_real_gamma.rs` (`DistributedRealHamiltonian`; in dft-physics because dft-solvers cannot depend on it, dft-mpi dependency added to dft-physics and ownership.toml). Row partition: representative i (ORC rows 2i-1, 2i; G=0 row 0) belongs to the owner of position `plus[i]`, local rows ascending in ORC index (identity for one rank). Mirror data: 2 extra alltoalls per apply (rows to the minus owner before the FFT, h[minus] back after), skipped on one rank. Per apply with m columns: alltoall = 2*ceil(m/2) + 2 (one rank: 2*ceil(m/2)), tree_sum = 1. Gate (`cargo test -p dft-physics --test distributed_real_gamma`): m = 1, 2, 3, 16, ranks 1..8, cubic, skewed and elongated cells with 8- and 2-projector species: max relative error vs `RealHamiltonian::apply_block` 1.3e-16 (one rank bit-identical); bit-reproducible per rank count. Left for M2c: adapter into the M1 Davidson (row-block algebra on `local_rows`), batching the per-pair FFT transposes across pairs, density and potential distribution (M2d).
- M2c implemented (2026-11-10): `RealDavidson::solve_distributed_rows` (crates/dft-solvers/src/distributed_davidson.rs) with the new trait `DistributedRealOperator` (`global_len`, `local_rows` = global ORC row of each local row, `apply_block(comm, x_local, y_local)`), implemented by `DistributedRealHamiltonian` (which also gained `update_from_complex` and keeps its pool). The M1 solver and the new one share one `solve_core`; the layout is now a per-rank list of global rows (contiguous for M1), the algebra helpers only use the local row count. Setup costs 2 small allgathervs (row counts, row lists); eigenvectors are gathered to the replicated ORC interface once at the end (plus 1 diagonal gather when there is no warm start). Engine: with a communicator of 2+ ranks and `BAANDI_MPI_M2=on` (default off; tests use the per-thread `parallel::set_m2_override`), `gamma_real.rs` builds the `DistributedRealHamiltonian` per k state, refreshes its potential every SCF step like the serial one, and calls the new solver (counter `parallel::distributed_rows_solves`); otherwise the M1 path (2+ ranks) or the threads-only path (1 rank) is unchanged. Gate (simulated ranks 2..5): eigenvalues within 1e-9 Ha of serial and independent residuals < 1e-8 on the real-H fixtures (cubic and skewed, with nonlocal projectors), a dense fixture and clustered spectra on a scattered (non-contiguous) row partition; all ranks bit-identical, bit-reproducible per rank count; engine SCF (cubic and skewed, 2 and 3 ranks) within 4.4e-16 to 6.7e-16 Ha of threads-only, iteration counts equal. Collectives per Davidson iteration (n=81, P=2..5, 18 iterations, `cargo test -p dft-physics --test distributed_real_gamma -- --nocapture`): M2c allgatherv 0.22 / tree_sum 33.5 / alltoall 7.8; M1 allgatherv 2.17 / tree_sum 32.4 / alltoall 0. M2c allgatherv total is 4 per solve independent of the iteration count; the alltoalls are the distributed FFT transposes (2 per column pair plus 2 mirror exchanges per apply). Not done: batching the per-pair transposes (it needs a multi-pair `DistributedPairFft` call and a larger staging buffer in the operator; judged too invasive for this stage). Left for M2d: distributing density, potential, mixing and the replicated stages, batched transposes, dev08 scaling gate before the default is switched.
- M2d implemented (2026-11-10), Mac correctness only, dev08 request `docs/performance/requests/mpi-m2d.md`. (1) Batched transposes: `DistributedPairFft::{begin_to_real_batch, to_real_plane_in, to_reciprocal_plane_in, finish_to_reciprocal_batch, apply_local_batch}` move all column pairs of a block with ONE alltoall per direction (transpose buffers grow to the widest block once); `DistributedRealHamiltonian` uses it by default (`set_batched`, engine switch `BAANDI_M2_BATCH=off` for A/B). Alltoall per apply of m columns: 2*ceil(m/2) + 2 before, 4 after (2 on one rank; the 2 mirror exchanges were already per block); m=16: 18 -> 4. Batched results are bit-identical to the per-pair code on cubic, skewed and elongated fixtures, m = 1, 3, 16, ranks 1, 2, 3, 5, 8. Davidson gate fixture (n=81, 18 iterations): alltoall per iteration 7.8 -> 4.2. (2) Increments complete: density (stick FFT of the distributed eigenvectors on each rank's planes, one batched transpose, planes assembled by one allgatherv; per-band plane contributions kept for the certificate screen, one tree_sum), potential (LDA PZ/PW XC by contiguous grid blocks, bit-identical potential, one tree_sum and one allgatherv; Hartree replicated), energies (audit nonlocal energy from the distributed projections, one tree_sum; output-density XC as above). Mixing is NOT distributed (0.3 percent of the si32 wall; a grid allgatherv per step would cost about what it saves). Out of the M2 path (replicated as before): GGA/PBE, NLCC, density floors, Fermi-Dirac, more than one k point. Gates: band densities equal the serial full-grid FFT within 7.4e-16 relative and are bit-identical for 1..6 ranks (the plane split is a permutation); nonlocal band energies within 7.7e-16 of `NonlocalOperator::expectation_values`; engine SCF (cubic and skewed cell, 2, 3, 4 ranks): strict dE <= 6.7e-16 Ha, standard dE <= 6.2e-11 Ha against threads-only, all M2d counters incremented, energy and density bit-reproducible at fixed rank count, every rank bit-equal; M2 requested on one rank stays bit-identical to threads-only. (3) Proof-of-branch counters and stage timers: `parallel::m2_stats()`, results.json `parallel.m2` (`counters`: rows_solves, batched_applies, unbatched_applies, applied_columns, band_density_builds, nonlocal_energy_evaluations, density_builds, xc_evaluations, nonlocal_energy_audits, screen_integrals; `stages`: engine density/xc/nonlocal_energy/screen_integrals and `op.*` apply/density/gather stages), `parallel.model = "mpi_stick_rows"` with M2 on.
