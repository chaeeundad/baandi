1. Complex Hermitian eigenproblem, faer vs LAPACK `zheev` (via `lapack::zheev`, link blas-src/lapack-src so Accelerate is used; the lapack crate calls are unsafe: confine them to one small module with preconditions documented):
   a. 2x2 [[2, 1-i],[1+i, 3]]: eigenvalues must be 1 and 4.
   b. 4x4 A = U D U^H with a fixed nontrivial unitary U (e.g. from a QR of a fixed matrix, or a product of Givens rotations with complex phases) and known diagonal D: recover D.
   c. seeded random Hermitian matrices of N = 100, 300, 600, 1000: report (i) max |lambda_faer - lambda_lapack|, (ii) residual max ||A v - lambda v||_2, (iii) orthonormality max |V^H V - I|, for BOTH libraries, (iv) wall time (release build, median of 3 runs after one warm-up), and how many threads each used if discoverable.
2. Complex gemm: faer matmul vs a naive triple loop, N = 64 (correctness, tolerance 1e-12 relative) and N = 500 (timing). Include one case with conjugate transpose of A.
3. 3D FFT with ndrustfft over complex data: grids 4x5x6 (against a naive O(N^2) 3D DFT written in the test), then 15x20x24, 16x16x16, 27x27x27, 32x32x32, 48x48x48. For each: round trip error after forward+inverse with the normalization you document, Parseval relative error, single plane-wave mode test (exp(i G.r) must produce a single nonzero coefficient at the correct wrapped index, including a negative frequency), and median wall time. Document exactly which normalization ndrustfft applies (forward unnormalized? inverse scaled?) by measurement, not by assumption. Check that planning/handler objects can be reused across calls.
