# dev08 request: B1 selected-root products, Davidson stage timers; B4 and B5 decisions (pre-registered 2026-11-10)

Source: proposal "Structural speed proposal" (2026-11-10, sections 1, 4 B1/B4/B5, 6). Ledger rows: B1-SELECTED-ROOTS, DAVIDSON-STAGES, B4-INNER, B5-BORDERED. Threads-only; no MPI file touched. Every rule below was written before any dev08 data and before the Mac harness data of B4 and the Mac timing of B5; rules are never moved after data.

## Switches (environment, read once per solve; default off; the default path is bit-identical)
- `BAANDI_DAVIDSON_STAGES=on`: clock reads plus relaxed atomic adds in the serial real Davidson (no I/O, no allocation). `results.json` -> `convergence.work.davidson_stages`: iteration wall time and counts by correction width (`0` = returning iteration, `1`, `2-8`, `9-64`, `65+`), and stage seconds (`reduced`, `eigen`, `rotate`, `residual`, `orthonormalize`, `h_apply` including certificate and restart applications, `sketch`, `precondition`), cumulative over the process. Zeros when off.
- `BAANDI_B1=standard|strict|on`: B1 selected-root products. `standard` acts on Ritz-change (standard-profile) solves, `strict` on residual (strict-profile) solves, `on` on both. Counters in `convergence.work.b1` (`narrow_iterations`, `full_iterations`, `narrow_columns`, `avoided_columns`, `fallbacks`, `sketch_estimates`, `sketch_disagreements`). Inactive when `BAANDI_SECOND_VECTOR` is set.

### What B1 does
In a narrow iteration (fewer than half the bands need work, not the iteration limit, no restart possible) only the needed roots get Ritz vectors, H images and residual vectors. Standard profile: the flags are Ritz-value changes, computed from the reduced eigenvalues as before, so every decision is unchanged; only GEMM column grouping changes (rounding class). Strict profile: a fixed-seed sign sketch S (s = min(128, capacity) rows, entries +-1/sqrt(s), seed derived from the solver seed) with A = S V and B = S HV maintained per appended column (rotated with the basis on a contraction restart, recomputed on a plain restart); est_j = ||B c_j - theta_j A c_j||. A root with est_j below half its threshold is flagged converged for that iteration; every other root gets the explicit residual and the exact test. If all explicit residuals pass, the iteration is redone on the all-band path; every return (and the iteration limit) is decided on the all-band path with explicit residuals and the unchanged in-solve certification. `history` in narrow iterations: standard = largest residual of the corrected roots; strict = largest of the explicit residuals and the estimates. Returned residuals are always explicit.

## Part B: B1 A/B (`BAANDI_B1=on` vs unset, timers off)
Cells: si16-gamma and si32-gamma, strict and standard, perturbed seeds 601-610 (+-1e-7 Bohr as in MPI-M1-GATE3), T=1 and T=8; generality cells (one seed each, 601, both profiles, T=1 and T=8): syn-spd, syn-p3, syn-d3 (synthetic multi-projector potentials) plus si-skew and si-slab (cell shapes).

Pre-registered acceptance (all must hold):
1. Accuracy: every run converged; |E(on) - E(off)| at most 1e-10 Ha (strict) and 5e-9 Ha (standard) per seed; density SHA-256 reproducible per setting and equal between T=1 and T=8 within the setting; strict returns certified as today (`fixed_point_certificate` outcomes unchanged in kind); `b1.sketch_disagreements` = 0 in every strict run (any non-zero value is reported with the seed; the switch fails if that seed's total Davidson work rises by more than 2 percent).
2. Work first: total Davidson iterations and total H vectors (recorded plus certificate, `convergence.work`): cell median within +-1 percent of off (B1 preserves decisions; a rounding-class trajectory move is expected on a few seeds), no single seed worse than +3 percent.
3. Premise: on si32 strict T=1, `b1.narrow_iterations` is at least 30 percent of all Davidson iterations; if not, the result is reported as "premise absent" and the wall rules are not evaluated.
4. Wall: median wall improves by at least 3 percent at T=1 and at least 2 percent at T=8 on si32 strict and on si32 standard; no cell median (si16, generality cells) slower than +1 percent; worst seed not worse than +3 percent.
Passing all four is a recommendation to make B1 the default in a separate decision with its own A/B of the default build. Failing any rule: rejected as registered, recorded in the ledger with the numbers; the switch stays opt-in.

### Part B addendum (2026-11-10, written after the Mac observations below and before any dev08 data): second arm `BAANDI_B1=on BAANDI_B1_SKETCH=count`
The Mac observation showed the dense sketch costs about as much as the rotation it saves (si32 strict: sketch 2.2 to 2.6 s against a rotate saving of about 3 s). `BAANDI_B1_SKETCH=count` replaces S by a CountSketch (one +-1 per coordinate in a seeded bucket; N adds per sketched column instead of s N; same expectation and the same variance bound 2 ||x||^4 / s of ||S x||^2; weaker tail bound for vectors dominated by a few coordinates). Both forms use S = identity when the space has no more coordinates than sketch rows (exact estimates). The count arm is measured in the same interleaved runs as a third setting (off / dense / count per seed) and judged by the same four rules, independently; if both pass, the one with the lower si32 strict T=1 median wall is recommended. Rule 1's disagreement clause matters most for the count arm.

## Part C: B5 A/B (`BAANDI_B5=on` vs unset, timers off; one extra timers-on run per cell for rule 4)
Same cells, seeds and protocol as part B. B5 is independent of B1 (measured alone).
1. Accuracy: as part B rule 1 (strict 1e-10 Ha, standard 5e-9 Ha, converged, SHA reproducible per setting and equal T=1 vs T=8, certificates unchanged in kind).
2. Work: cell median of total Davidson iterations and H vectors within +-1 percent of off; no seed worse than +3 percent.
3. Mechanism: `b5.bordered` at least 30 percent of the Davidson iterations on si32 strict, and `b5.declined` at most 1 percent of the bordered attempts (bordered plus declined) in every cell.
4. Stage: with `BAANDI_DAVIDSON_STAGES=on`, the median `eigen` stage drops by at least 25 percent on si32 strict at T=1 and at T=8.
5. Wall: median wall improves by at least 1.5 percent at T=8 on si32 strict; no cell median slower than +1 percent; worst seed not worse than +3 percent.
Pass: recommendation to the orchestrator (default change is a separate decision). Fail: rejected as registered, ledger row, stays opt-in.

## B4 (inner iterations for narrow blocks): Mac harness decision rule
No engine code. `#[ignore]` harness `crates/dft-solvers/tests/b4_inner_iterations.rs` (one Davidson written for the test, run as baseline and as the sequential-directions variant on the same fixtures: kinetic-coupled and clustered spectra with a strict prefix). The frozen-Hamiltonian replay (`dft_solvers::replay`) captures only the complex path, so captured si32 real tail solves are not available.
Rule (fixed before the harness ran): B4 earns a dev08 engine prototype only if, on at least two harness fixtures whose baseline has at least 30 percent single-column iterations, the variant (a) reduces full Rayleigh-Ritz steps by at least 25 percent (median over fixtures), (b) raises H columns by at most 10 percent on every fixture, and (c) lowers the modelled cost H + c x steps by at least 15 percent with c = 33 H-equivalents per full step (proposal F1, a D number for si32 strict) and does not raise it with c = 10. Otherwise it is recorded as rejected on the Mac fixture. Mac fixtures are not the si32 tail; a rejection is "not supported by the fixture", and a pass is only the reason to write a prototype and its own dev08 registration.

## B5 (bordered update of the reduced eigenproblem): Mac gate
Implemented only if the existing reduced eigensolve (`RealEighWorkspace::solve_into`, faer on the Mac build) exceeds 5 percent of the Davidson iteration cost on this Mac build, measured (a) by a microbenchmark at orders up to 408 and (b) by the `eigen` stage share of the summed Davidson iteration time (`davidson_stages`) on si16 and si32 strict, T=1. Otherwise recorded as "not worth it on the Mac numbers, needs the dev08 EVD share (part A)". The dev08 part A answers the question for MKL.
