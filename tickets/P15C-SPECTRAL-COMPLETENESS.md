TASK 1 (reproduce and scope, no behaviour change): (a) build an independent dense oracle (test-only): the full Hamiltonian matrix at one k point by applying the production H to a complete basis (or to unit vectors), then a dense Hermitian eigensolve with a different code path (faer evd or LAPACK-free reference); sizes up to a few thousand plane waves; (b) for the FINAL converged state of each of: Si 8-atom Gamma, Si k444s (all k points or a sample), the 2-atom cases, PseudoDojo bulk Si and MgO fixtures of P13D (if DFT_NC_DIR is readable), the 20-atom skewed, 4-atom elongated, slab-like and 16-atom synthetic fixtures of P15A, and spin-polarized synthetic case of P13B: compare the solver's returned lowest nbands eigenvalues (occupied, the first empty band, buffer bands separately) with the dense spectrum of the same final Hamiltonian, and report the maximum discrepancy, the missing eigenvalue indices and their degeneracy pattern; also compute the total energy error that missing occupied states would imply (band sum difference); (c) show at which SCF iteration the discrepancy appears and whether it disappears by itself in later iterations (starting from previous vectors); (d) identify the mechanism: starting vectors without overlap to some low eigenvectors (lowest kinetic energy plane waves as start), degenerate manifolds, Davidson corrections that cannot create components in directions orthogonal to the span (classic missed eigenvector), preconditioner effects; report with evidence.
