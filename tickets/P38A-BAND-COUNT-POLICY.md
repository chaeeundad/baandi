GOAL: Study and, if justified, add an opt-in automatic policy for the number of extra bands above the occupied ones. Read docs/performance/band-count-scan.md (orchestrator scan: work versus extra bands, degeneracy-dependent optimum), the dev08 Q1 (a) section of tests/reference/perf/dev08_materials_qe_relayed.md (QE nbnd = occupied: -20 to -26 percent on si16 and si32, no gain on si8), docs/performance/qe-parity-plan.md, the ledger (P7PC top-band degeneracy regression rows, A08, A13, A21, P31A), AGENT-BRIEF.md, benchmarks/cases/si8-gamma.toml. Facts: BAANDI's default is occupied + 4 extra bands (`scf.extra_bands`, preflight.rs); on the three small cells fewer extra bands need fewer H vectors (k444s extra 1: 7574 versus 10290 at the default, -26 percent), but on si8 a cut inside the degenerate top multiplet stagnates the Davidson and completing the multiplet (extra 6 to 10) cuts the H vectors by 40 to 50 percent (1042 at extra 8 versus 2063). The strict profile certifies the occupied bands plus the first empty band; higher bands are buffers.
PHASE A (study, no behaviour change): a table over the systems below of Davidson steps, H vectors, SCF iterations, wall time (T=1, median of at least 3 runs; note the machine load) and the energy difference to the default-band result, as a function of extra bands (all integers from 1 to 12, plus the first value that completes the degenerate multiplet above the last occupied band if different), for strict and standard profiles. Systems (generality rule, not silicon only): the benchmark cases gamma, k222, k222s, k444s, si8, si16-gamma (benchmarks/cases), si-skew, si-elongated, si-slab, synthetic fixtures with different projector structures and gaps (benchmarks/cases syn-*), the PseudoDojo MgO and Si materials (../rust-dft-wt/pseudo/pseudodojo, never copy or commit UPF files). For each case record the eigenvalue spectrum at the converged state around the top occupied band (the gaps between the bands from occupied to occupied+12) and whether the multiplet containing the first empty band is complete at each band count. Find out whether the degeneracy-completion effect is explained by the multiplet structure (state the evidence) and whether it also appears on non-cubic cells.
PHASE B (only if Phase A shows a rule that helps broadly): implement `[scf] extra_bands = "auto"` (default stays the current integer 4, bit-identical) with a deterministic rule whose inputs are available before the SCF starts or after its first iteration (for example the eigenvalue gaps of the first Davidson solve, or the free-electron spectrum), documented and tested, with a fallback to the current default when the rule cannot decide; the rule must not depend on the thread count; the number of bands must be identical across k points (state how).
