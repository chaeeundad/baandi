GOAL: Test a hypothesis that would explain a reference-code behaviour seen on dev08 (tests/reference/perf/dev08_materials_qe_relayed.md, sections on (5) k-point symmetry and its correction/additions; read them, then docs/validation/symmetry.md, docs/decisions for the symmetry design (ADR list), crates/dft-physics/src/symmetry.rs, the symmetry hooks in crates/dft-engine/src/scf.rs, AGENT-BRIEF.md and the ledger; do NOT look for or read any QE source). Facts: on the shifted 4x4x4 Si mesh (k444s) BAANDI reduces 64 full mesh points to 10 irreducible points using only the 12 crystal operations that map the k mesh onto itself and symmetrizes the density with those 12; sym and off then agree to 1.78e-14 Ha. The reference code reduced the same mesh to 10 points with the same weights, but its total energy with symmetry on differs from its own symmetry-off energy by 1.554e-05 Ha (energy lower with symmetry), while on the unshifted mesh (invariant under all 48 crystal operations) sym and off agree to 1.9e-12 Ha; the effect is independent of the FFT grid (25 or 32), of time reversal, and of convergence. HYPOTHESIS: the reference code symmetrizes the density with ALL 48 crystal operations even though only 12 map the shifted mesh onto itself. The mesh density has only the 12-operation symmetry; averaging it over all 48 operations gives a different density, equivalent to sampling the union of the rotated meshes (a denser effective k sampling), which changes the energy by an amount of that order; on an invariant mesh the two procedures coincide. This ticket implements the full-group density symmetrization as a research option and tests the prediction without needing any convention offsets, because the offset between the codes cancels in differences.
OPTION: `[symmetry] density_group = "mesh" | "crystal"` (default `mesh` = today, bit-identical). `crystal`: the k-point reduction and weights stay exactly as today (mesh-preserving operations only), but the output density symmetrization (and the corresponding input-density invariance check, if any) uses every detected crystal operation that is realizable as a permutation on the FFT grid (fractional translations must be commensurate with the grid; report which operations are used and refuse with a clear error if some detected operation is not realizable, never silently drop it). Include the option in the exact-restart comparison and result metadata. Applies only when symmetry mode is auto; anything else with `crystal` is an error.
