Difficulty 1 is a small adapter; 5 is a new numerical subsystem. Effort estimates are **agent-days** of implementation, local testing and documentation, with architect/scientific review and remote queue time additional. ## 1. Collinear spin polarization: LSDA and spin GGA

**Established physics.** Use independent spin orbitals and occupations `0 <= f_nks <= 1`, with weights summing to one:

\[
n_s(r)=\sum_{kn}w_k f_{nks}|\psi_{nks}(r)|^2,\quad
n=n_\uparrow+n_\downarrow,\quad m=n_\uparrow-n_\downarrow,\quad
M/\mu_B=\int m(r)\,dr.
\]

The last expression defines the reported spin-polarization convention; electron magnetic-moment sign and spin axis must be documented separately. Hartree depends on total n. LSDA uses `E_xc = integral n epsilon_xc(n,zeta)`, `zeta=m/n`. Exchange obeys `E_x[n_up,n_down] = (E_x[2n_up]+E_x[2n_down])/2`. For GGA with energy density e and `sigma_st = grad n_s dot grad n_t`,

\[
v_{xc,\uparrow}=e_{n_\uparrow}-\nabla\cdot
(2e_{\sigma_{uu}}\nabla n_\uparrow+e_{\sigma_{ud}}\nabla n_\downarrow),
\]

with the analogous down expression. The mixed-gradient term has no extra factor two. PZ81 and PW92 are different correlation models [1,2].

**Code, representation, audit.** Add a collinear spin mode, per-(spin,k) eigenpairs/occupations and two density channels; remove the even-electron restriction only in the new spin-capable path. Introduce a spin XC result instead of treating `XcGrid.potential` as universal. Update `xc.rs`, `gga.rs`, `density.rs`, Hamiltonian assembly, both `scf.rs` steps, mixer layout and convergence certificate. Mix charge and magnetization residuals with separately tunable preconditioning; project charge conservation without erasing a freely relaxing magnetization. Enforce `n_up,n_down >= 0` within declared numerical tolerances. Audit `sum_s integral n_s v_xc,s`; common Hartree/ion terms occur once, not once per spin. Distinguish unconstrained common chemical potential from fixed total magnetization (two spin populations/Lagrange multipliers).

**ESLE/coupling.** Add `spin_mode`, quantization axis, spin-density payloads, per-spin spectra/f, total and local moments, local-moment partition definition, initial atomic moments and any magnetization constraint. Model fields include constraint policy; initial moments are provenance when they only seed the same unconstrained problem. Store AFM patterns, not just total M (zero total M does not mean nonmagnetic). Distances compare m and local moments in a declared common frame. Coupling needs a typed spin-conjugate field and its energy/derivative convention before Zeeman-like inputs; an ordinary scalar field couples to n.

**Dependencies, libraries, validation.** Reuse polarized xcx PBE and PW92. Recommend library LSDA-PW92 first, explicitly named, retaining scalar PZ81; a polarized PZ81 adapter is a separate choice needing published PZ81 derivative tests or a reviewed library. NLCC and ONCV coverage (§9) are practical prerequisites for chosen oxide/metal fixtures. Synthetic spin scaling, spin swap invariance, full polarization, zero-m limit, charge/M integrals and discrete directional derivatives precede QE: Si forced to zero spin, then FM Fe and AFM NiO with matched NC data. QE moments must use the same spatial/projector partition to compare local values. No oxide numerical reference is established by this task.

**Risks / estimate / order.** Spin-channel mixing instabilities, accidental time-reversal assumptions, core-valence XC and metastable moment collapse. **10–16 agent-days**, plus **3–5** for polarized PZ81 if selected; order **1**, essential for magnetism and meaningful ESLE phase E. Spin alone does not make a Mott gap reliable.

## 2. Smearing, Fermi levels, and metallic k convergence

**Established physics.** Let `x=(epsilon-mu)/eta`, with width eta in Ha. Occupation `f(x)` is per spin state; the scalar mode has explicit degeneracy g=2. Solve `sum_kns w_k f_nks = N`. Fermi–Dirac has `f=1/(1+exp(x))`, eta=`k_B T`; Gaussian has `f=erfc(x)/2`. Methfessel–Paxton (MP) uses a Hermite-corrected delta [3]:

\[
D_p(x)=e^{-x^2}\sum_{j=0}^{p}\frac{(-1)^j H_{2j}(x)}{4^j j!\sqrt\pi},\qquad
f_p(x)=\int_x^\infty D_p(t)dt.
\]

Marzari–Vanderbilt (MV, cold smearing) uses a shifted, moment-corrected Gaussian [4]. One convention consistent with x above is

\[
f_{MV}(x)=\tfrac12\operatorname{erfc}(x+1/\sqrt2)
+\frac{e^{-(x+1/\sqrt2)^2}}{\sqrt{2\pi}}.
\]

Implement its entropy-like correction from the **same** occupation primitive. More generally define `s'(x)=x f'(x)`, `s(infinity)=0`; then `F_eta = E - eta sum w s(x)` is stationary under the associated occupations. For Gaussian `s=e^(-x^2)/(2 sqrt(pi))`; for FD `s=-f ln f-(1-f)ln(1-f)` and `F=E-TS`. Only FD represents physical electronic entropy. MP/MV corrections are quadrature devices; neither the zero-width extrapolated energy nor Gaussian width is automatically a physical temperature. MP can give negative occupations; MV can exceed unity under this convention. Never silently clamp these schemes to FD bounds.

**Code, audit, representation.** New occupation evaluator/root solver in the engine or solver layer, used by both SCF paths and the final certificate; `OccupationPolicy` becomes an explicit policy with width/order, degeneracy and electron-number tolerance. Store f **per k and spin**, not one common vector. Expand empty bands until occupation tails and electron-count bracketing converge; occupied-only residual gates must include significantly weighted extra bands. For monotone FD/Gaussian, bracket mu then safeguarded bisection/Newton; test zero-width/gap and degeneracy limits. MP/MV need a documented root-selection search/continuation policy because N(mu) can be nonmonotone. Start with FD/Gaussian. Their negative/overshooting occupation weights also invalidate positivity assumptions and the old `sum f*r` residual bound: use an appropriate absolute-weight bound and reject unsafe densities, not relaxed tolerances.

Add `internal_energy`, `entropy_term` (Ha), `free_energy`, mu and optional explicitly defined zero-width estimate to `EnergyAudit`. Use free-energy convergence, charge residual and weighted orbital residuals together. Derivatives for forces/stress must differentiate the same F at fixed N and width; entropy/occupation stationarity cancels the corresponding implicit variations. A zero-temperature extrapolation is not generally the function differentiated by finite-width forces.

**ESLE/coupling.** Fingerprint smearing type, eta, MP order, electron number and fixed-M policy; store per-spin mu only when constrained, temperature only for FD, entropy and F. Use `C diag(f) C†` for fractional-occupation distances. A fixed-N solver does not supply a grand-canonical electrode API: fixed-mu needs `Omega=F-mu*N` and a separate charge/background contract. Reject that combination until designed.

**Dependencies/libraries/oracles.** Existing libm for erf/exp and faer infrastructure; no specialist dependency needed for these physics formulas. Validate analytic two-level/free-electron cases, entropy derivatives, charge conservation, shift invariance and insulating limit. QE 7.6 [Q]: Al for all four supported smearings, then Fe after spin; compare mu after potential-reference alignment, occupations, internal E and entropy separately. Sweep both **k mesh and eta** (e.g. proposed 4³, 8³, 12³ and 0.02, 0.01, 0.005 Ha; these are starting experiments, not converged settings). Refine k at each width, then extrapolate width; report magnetic moments and energy differences as well as total energy. No “metal convergence” from a single broadened coarse mesh.

**Risks / estimate / order.** False convergence from insufficient empty states, multiple roots, spin transitions, and cancellation in entropy tails. **8–12 agent-days** FD/Gaussian, **4–7** MP/MV and regression sweeps. Order **2**, sharing the representation design with spin and preceding metallic oxides/alloys.

## 3. Dudarev DFT+U and ESLE occupation control

**Established physics.** For an explicitly orthonormal correlated subspace on site I [5],

\[
n^{Is}_{mm'}=\sum_{kn}w_k f_{nks}
\langle\phi^I_m|\psi_{nks}\rangle\langle\psi_{nks}|\phi^I_{m'}\rangle,
\quad E_U=\sum_{Is}\frac{U^I_{eff}}2\operatorname{Tr}[n^{Is}-(n^{Is})^2],
\]
\[
U_{eff}=U-J,\qquad V_U^{Is}=U_{eff}^I(\tfrac12 I-n^{Is}).
\]

This is the simplified rotationally invariant correction with a fully localized limit double-counting convention already incorporated. Do not add a second double-counting subtraction to E_U. Distinct U,J anisotropy, intersite V and DMFT are not delivered by Dudarev.

**Projectors and code.** Parse and expose normalized **PP_PSWFC / PP_CHI** atomic pseudo-wavefunctions through a validated dft-upf accessor, retaining radial measure, angular momentum and transform conventions. Kleinman–Bylander beta projectors are scattering operators, not interchangeable Hubbard orbitals. Files lacking suitable atomic wavefunctions must fail with a capability error. Recommend an explicit all-selected-orbital Löwdin orthogonalization `phi_tilde=phi S^(-1/2)` per Bloch k, with conditioning checks, as one reproducible option; raw atomic projectors are a distinct model. Document whether intersite overlaps are included. Implement projection/occupation and separable V_U action in a new `dft-physics` module; add SCF matrix updates, matrix residuals and orbital rotation tests. Energy band audit subtracts `sum_Is Tr(n_out V_U,in)` and adds E_U[n_out]. Re-derive the orbital-dependent fixed-point remainder. Derivatives of moving/orthogonalized projectors are required for +U forces.

**ESLE/coupling.** New model fields: sites/shells, radial-projector hashes, local frames, overlap/orthogonalization policy, U_eff in Ha, double-counting ID. Solution fields: Hermitian complex occupation matrices, eigenoccupations, moments and matrix residuals. Phase E can seed orbital order and explore different occupation branches. Temporary occupation constraints are recorded with their release schedule; only fully released, converged states of the same Hamiltonian can be ranked as unconstrained solutions. Persistent constraints define another model. External occupation potentials require nonlocal matrix coupling and a named constraint-energy owner; a scalar-grid coupling contract cannot encode them.

**Dependencies/libraries/oracles.** Spin, fractional occupations, ONCV parser, ESLE B and existing faer Hermitian eigensolvers. No new numerical library is necessary for the small matrices. Analytic gates: E_U=0 for idempotent occupations, U_eff=0 recovers baseline, Hermiticity, subspace-unitary invariance, finite-difference orbital derivative. QE on dev08: matched `atomic` or `ortho-atomic` Hubbard definition, then AFM NiO and a Fe/Mn oxide; compare matrices after basis/frame mapping, correction energy and moments. Different projector definitions make equal numeric U physically different; do not force total-energy equality across those models. Published NiO results [5] are trends, not an exact NC fixture.

**Risks / estimate / order.** Nonorthogonal populations outside [0,1], basis dependence of U, trapping in metastable states, missing semicore charge and double counting. **14–24 agent-days** for energies/SCF and phase-E starts, **6–10** more for +U derivatives. Order **3**; highest immediate return for transition-metal oxides/cathodes. cRPA is not a prerequisite: start with explicitly sourced user parameters and sensitivity sweeps. Linear-response U is another later option, distinct from cRPA.

## 4. Meta-GGA: SCAN and r2SCAN

**Established physics.** SCAN adds positive orbital kinetic-energy density; r2SCAN regularizes numerically difficult interpolation while restoring constraints [6,7]. For periodic parts u with the repository normalization,

\[
\tau_s(r)=\tfrac12\sum_{kn}w_k f_{nks}|(\nabla+ik)u_{nks}(r)|^2,
\quad E_{xc}=\int e(n_s,\sigma_{st},\tau_s)dr.
\]

Besides the spin GGA multiplicative potential, generalized KS requires

\[
\hat V_{\tau,s}u=-\tfrac12(\nabla+ik)\cdot[v_{\tau,s}(r)(\nabla+ik)u],
\quad v_{\tau,s}=\partial e/\partial\tau_s.
\]

It is not a scalar potential obtained by adding vtau to vrho. The factor 1/2 must match the library tau convention.

**Code/audit.** New tau builder and meta-GGA adapter in `dft-physics`, spectral k+G gradients with correct adjoints, an operator-coefficient field in the Hamiltonian, generalized functional evaluation inputs in `functional.rs`, and orbital-dependent input/output tracking in `scf.rs`. Spin-resolved n, f and tau must refer to the same orbitals; mixing only n while lagging tau without a convergence criterion is insufficient. In the band audit subtract the tau operator expectation `sum_s integral v_tau,s tau_s` as well as multiplicative n*v terms, then add E_xc once. Test orbital derivatives including changes of tau. Do not claim the old density-only bound.

**ESLE/coupling.** Record functional revision, threshold/regularization policy, tau definition and per-spin tau (or enough orbitals to reconstruct it), tau/operator residual and orbital hashes. Orbital-free archives cannot resume a full meta-GGA state without reinitialization. Generalized KS capability must be explicit to callers; current-dependent or magnetic-vector-potential extensions need gauge-invariant kinetic-density treatment and are deferred. External scalar fields retain their original role; they do not modify vtau by convention.

**Dependencies/libraries/oracles.** Recommend **r2SCAN first** through existing xcx 497/498; original SCAN requires a reviewed separate implementation/library, preferably libxc (MPL-2.0, conditional approval; §5). Check first derivatives against directional differences and second derivatives before DFPT. Uniform electron gas tau, single-orbital limit, rigid translations, Hermiticity and Bloch relabeling precede Si and oxide calculations. QE 7.6 may provide SCAN/r2SCAN through built-in or linked libxc paths, but the remote executable's functional availability and tau convention were not verified. Without that capability, library-level and variational tests exist, but no same-UPF material oracle is available yet. Matching a library shared by both codes is not fully independent validation.

**Risks / estimate / order.** Low-density singularities, aliasing, inconsistent tau screening, SCAN integration sensitivity, XC–pseudopotential inconsistency, magnetic over-stabilization. **14–22 agent-days** r2SCAN; **5–9** additional for approved SCAN adapter; **6–10** for forces/stress. Order **6**, after useful spin/+U/geometry work; independent of EXX and cRPA.

## 5. Hybrid functionals: plane-wave EXX, HSE, and ACE

**Established physics.** Exchange is a nonlocal same-spin occupied-pair interaction:

\[
E_x^{EXX}=-\tfrac12\sum_{s,kn,k'm}w_kw_{k'}f_{nks}f_{mk's}
\iint\psi^*_{nks}(r)\psi_{mk's}(r)\,v(r-r')\,
\psi^*_{mk's}(r')\psi_{nks}(r')\,dr\,dr'.
\]

For HSE [8], `1/r = erfc(omega r)/r + erf(omega r)/r`,

\[
E_{xc}^{HSE}=aE_x^{EXX,SR}+(1-a)E_x^{PBE,SR}
+E_x^{PBE,LR}+E_c^{PBE}.
\]

HSE06 normally uses a=0.25 and omega approximately 0.11 Bohr^-1; retain the exact selected variant/parameter units. In reciprocal space `v_SR(Q)=4*pi*(1-exp(-Q²/(4 omega²)))/Q²`, with finite Q=0 limit `pi/omega²`. Full EXX has an integrable singularity requiring a separately validated k-integration/auxiliary-function treatment; dropping its head without a correction is not converged exchange. PBE0 uses unscreened EXX; HSE also needs **screened semilocal exchange**, absent from current xcx.

**Code, audit, representation.** Implement pair-density FFTs at momentum transfer k−k′, separate exchange grid/cutoff, cached occupied orbitals and a matrix-free Fock action in `dft-physics`. Preserve momentum/basis mappings across per-k G spheres; reconstruct full k sets before assuming scalar time-reversal reductions apply to pair sums. Extend `functional.rs` into a composition specification (semilocal, exact-exchange fraction and range), with explicit unsupported errors. `scf.rs` needs outer Fock refresh/inner eigensolve convergence. For self-consistent exchange, `sum w f <psi|V_x|psi> = 2 E_x`; subtract that expectation and add E_x in the energy audit. Record the exact operator used for eigenpairs, rather than evaluating band corrections with an inconsistent refreshed exchange.

ACE [9] compresses the action on a reference subspace: with columns W=V_x C and M=C†W, `V_ACE=W M^(-1) W†` on a nonsingular reference span. Use stable factorizations, not an explicit inverse; it reproduces V_x C but not arbitrary states. Rebuild when occupied orbitals change, expand the span for needed empty states, and certify the final energy/residual with full exchange. ACE reduces repeated applications, not the first pair build or the asymptotic pair count.

**ESLE/coupling.** Fingerprint a, omega, screened semilocal recipe, exchange cutoff, k/q sampling and singularity policy. Store occupied orbitals/density matrices and exact-exchange ledger; ACE factors are optional numerical checkpoint data tied to an orbital hash, not intrinsic state identity. External nonlocal terms need an operator interface and energy ownership. Hybrid derivative support is a separate capability; do not label semilocal forces as hybrid forces.

## 6. Dispersion: D3, D4, vdW-DF, and rVV10

**Established physics.** D3 adds damped pair dispersion (typically n=6,8) and optionally three-body Axilrod–Teller–Muto energy [10]:

\[
E_{disp}=-\sum_{A<B,\,images}\sum_{n=6,8}
s_n C_n^{AB} f_{damp,n}(R_{AB})/R_{AB}^n + E_{ATM}.
\]

Periodic summation needs unique pair/image counting. D3 coefficients depend on coordination; D4 adds atomic-charge-dependent polarizabilities [11], often with a separate electronegativity-equilibration charge model. This charge is not automatically the engine's DFT density partition. vdW-DF and rVV10 instead add a nonlocal density correlation [12,13]:

\[
E_c^{nl}=\tfrac12\iint n(r)\Phi[n](r,r')n(r')\,dr\,dr',
\quad v_c^{nl}=\delta E_c^{nl}/\delta n.
\]

rVV10 has a simpler kernel and damping parameters tied to the semilocal parent. It is not equivalent to a pair D3 correction, nor is the nonlocal kernel provided by an XC functional name alone.

**Code/audit/state/coupling.** Geometry-only D3 or EEQ-D4 can be evaluated outside the SCF and added once to both total-energy forms as `dispersion`; no electronic Hamiltonian or occupation change for that model. Add geometry derivatives including coordination/charge response before claiming forces or stress. A density-derived D4 charge prescription would need an explicit variational chain rule in SCF. For vdW-DF/rVV10, new nonlocal-correlation FFT/kernel module, SCF potential and `energy.rs` correction must include subtraction of `integral n v_c_nl` in the band audit; existing spin/occupations are reused, with the functional's documented spin dependence. ESLE fingerprints parent functional, damping, ATM switch, cutoff/image convention, parameter version/hash, charge model and nonlocal kernel version. Store correction energies and atomic charges when used. Coupling specifies whether engine or external driver owns dispersion; never add it twice through an ML/environment correction. Unsupported derivative combinations return unavailable.

## 7. RPA correlation through ACFDT

**Established physics.** This is a post-Kohn–Sham many-body energy, not “an RPA LDA” and not a quasiparticle solver. At zero electronic temperature, with a trace over space/basis and a Brillouin-zone q average implicit [14–18],

\[
E_c^{RPA}=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^\infty d\omega\,
\operatorname{Tr}\{\ln[1-\chi^0(i\omega)v]+\chi^0(i\omega)v\}.
\]

For the periodic calculation use `sum_q w_q` explicitly. The Adler–Wiser independent-particle response is

\[
\chi^0_{GG'}(q,i\omega)=\frac1\Omega\sum_{knms}w_k
\frac{f_{nks}-f_{m,k+q,s}}{i\omega+\epsilon_{nks}-\epsilon_{m,k+q,s}}
M^G_{nmks}(q)M^{G'*}_{nmks}(q),
\]
\[
M^G_{nmks}(q)=\langle\psi_{nks}|e^{-i(q+G)\cdot r}|\psi_{m,k+q,s}\rangle.
\]

Here f is per spin (or use scalar occupations with degeneracy exactly once). Include both transition directions consistently; an occupied–empty implementation on the imaginary axis must reproduce this expression, not accidentally double it. `v_G(q)=4*pi/|q+G|²`. Evaluate via the Hermitian symmetrized `A=sqrt(v) chi0 sqrt(v)`: `Tr[ln(I-A)+A] = sum_j [log(1-lambda_j)+lambda_j]`. For a stable imaginary-axis response, lambda_j <= 0 and the correlation integrand is nonpositive. Use stable `log1p` and cancellation handling, and solve/factor rather than explicitly invert dielectric matrices.

The **response plane-wave cutoff** `|q+G|²/2 <= E_chi` is distinct from wavefunction and charge-density cutoffs. Product FFTs must resolve the response coefficients. At q=0 the head/wings require an optical small-q limit, including the nonlocal pseudopotential contribution to velocity/matrix elements (or independently converged finite-q treatment). Deleting G=0 is a finite-size prescription with bias, not the physical optical limit. For metals, intraband response and thermal occupation limits need another gate; first RPA milestone is insulating Si.

**Convergence and scaling.** Explicit transitions scale roughly `O(N_k N_occ N_empty N_Gchi² N_omega)` for dense accumulation: **O(N^4)** in system size when each band/basis count grows linearly. Matrix factorization costs `O(N_q N_omega N_Gchi³)`; FFT transition construction adds its own cost. Faster cubic/low-rank methods exist, but are not the first correctness implementation. Stream q/frequency blocks and transitions; one dense complex128 response matrix uses `16*N_Gchi²` bytes: **16 MB at 1,000 G**, **400 MB at 5,000 G**, **1.6 GB at 10,000 G**. Several matrices/workspaces and orbital storage multiply those numbers. Do not allocate all q and frequencies at once.

Use mapped Gauss–Legendre integration, e.g. `omega=omega0*(1+x)/(1-x)` on x in [-1,1], including the Jacobian; vary omega0 and node count. Minimax quadratures are a later alternative, with source/license and fitted spectral range recorded, not copied numerical tables. Empty bands must converge jointly with E_chi and the **orbital** cutoff; “number of empty bands equals occupied bands” is not a criterion. Hundreds of empty states can be needed even for primitive Si, and the eventual count is determined by tests. An `E_c(E_chi)=E_c(infinity)+A E_chi^(-3/2)` fit is an asymptotic convergence diagnostic [18], not permission to fit unconverged band sums or q sampling.

**Total energy and code changes.** On a fixed semilocal KS reference:

\[
E^{RPA}=\underbrace{T_s+E_{ion,local}+E_{ion,nonlocal}+E_H+E_{ion-ion}}_{E_{KS,nonxc}}
+E_x^{EXX}+E_c^{RPA}
=E_{KS}-E_{xc}^{KS}+E_x^{EXX}+E_c^{RPA}.
\]

Evaluate every term on the same reference density/orbitals. This is **unscreened** exact exchange, not HSE's fraction. Starting with a semilocal reference avoids ambiguous removal of prior +U/hybrid terms; reject those combinations initially. A non-self-consistent RPA energy is not the KS eigenvalue sum and is not stationary with respect to the reference orbitals. Its forces are not ordinary Hellmann–Feynman KS forces.

Add a frozen-potential empty-state solve in the engine; expand eigensolver capacity and residual reporting for all retained empty states. New response basis/mappings in `dft-basis`, transition and dielectric modules in `dft-physics`, and postprocessing orchestration in `dft-engine`. Existing `extra_bands` is not sufficient: the SCF occupied-residual criterion does not certify high empty eigenpairs. Extend the audit with reference E_KS, removed E_xc, E_EXX and E_c_RPA, postprocessing tolerances and per-term convergence; do not overwrite the SCF convergence certificate. Reuse faer eigensolvers/factorizations, rustfft and quadrature physics glue; no new permissive turnkey plane-wave RPA library was verified. Published algorithms are implemented independently, never from QE source.

**ESLE/coupling.** Archive parent state/orbital hashes, KS starting functional, full k and q meshes, response basis, empty-state count/energy ceiling and residuals, frequency nodes/weights, Coulomb-head policy, frozen-core treatment and convergence uncertainty. Density and occupations remain those of the KS reference; report a separate post-KS evaluated energy. State comparisons need identical postprocessing model/accuracy contracts. Coupled RPA energy is initially unavailable when the environment adds screening: vacuum v and an environment-screened kernel are different physical models. No RPA forces, spectra or outer stationary solution are implied by a completed energy integral.

### Minimal Si proof of concept (proposal)

1. Build a tiny synthetic two-level response and uniform electron gas quadrature. Check transition Hermiticity, high-frequency `chi0 ~ omega^-2`, negative-semidefinite response, zero-interaction E_c=0 and direct scalar trace-log versus matrix factorization. Verify state gauge invariance.
2. Use a provenance-verified NC Si potential, primitive 2-atom cell, fixed occupations, scalar LDA or PBE reference. Store full orbitals/eigenvalues and independently certify empty states. Begin **Gamma-centered 1³, 2³, 3³, 4³** grids with compatible q differences; treat 1³ as a debugging limit, not bulk convergence. Initial full-grid implementation avoids response-symmetry mistakes.
3. Proposed convergence ladder: response cutoffs **2, 4, 6, 8 Ha**, empty-band counts **32, 64, 128, 256**, frequency counts **16, 32, 64**; raise orbital cutoff as needed. These are experiments, not claimed adequate thresholds. At fixed q and E_chi first converge bands and frequency; then increase E_chi and q jointly, testing extrapolation only in its asymptotic regime. Demonstrate head/wing treatment separately.
4. Build unscreened EXX independently and combine the ledger above. Keep EXX k convergence separate from response q convergence; if using different meshes, document how each term approaches the same continuum limit and do not subtract unmatched raw E_KS terms.
5. Compute an E(V) curve with 5–7 volumes only after energy convergence, fit an equation of state and infer a0 and B0. Set error budgets from the curvature signal; a proposed **1 meV/atom** relative-energy target must be demonstrated, not treated as an existing result. Publish absolute E_c as well as volume differences and uncertainties.

### Oracles and their limits

- **Uniform electron gas:** use the continuum Lindhard response in the scalar ACFDT integral, `n=3/(4*pi*r_s³)`, to generate an independent curve at proposed r_s=0.5,1,2,5,10 Bohr (r_s conventionally quoted as a dimensionless radius in units of a0). Compare with the **RPA** data/fit of Vosko, Wilk and Nusair (1980) [19], not their QMC fit or PZ81. Existing xcx `LdaCVwnRpa` provides a secondary fit check, not an independent implementation oracle. Gell-Mann–Brueckner (1957) and Ziesche (2007) [20,21] constrain the high-density logarithmic slope `(1-ln 2)/pi² ≈ 0.031091 Ha/electron`. The direct-ring RPA constant is about **−0.0711 Ha/electron**; do not substitute the constant including second-order exchange. Finite-r_s reference tabulations were not transcribed/verified here; obtain and archive them before a numerical acceptance gate.
- **Published Si correlation:** Nguyen and de Gironcoli (2009), Table II [16], report **E_c = −6.12 eV/atom at a=10.20 Bohr**, **−6.11 eV/atom at a=10.26 Bohr** for EXX/RPA; Table II and its surrounding normalization must accompany the future fixture. Bruneval's own 2014 calculation reports **−6.094 eV/atom at 10.26 Bohr** [22]. These differ in numerical/pseudopotential treatments; they are sanity ranges, not rust-dft expected values. The original Nguyen table distinguishes the two lattice constants; do not conflate them using a rounded secondary summary. RPA correlation is more negative than QMC here; QMC is not the RPA implementation oracle.
- **Lattice constant and bulk modulus:** Harl, Schimka and Kresse (2010), *Assessing the quality of the random phase approximation for lattice constants and atomization energies of solids* [17], provides solid equation-of-state benchmarks including Si; Harl and Kresse (2009) [18] provides a related bulk-property reference. Exact Si a0/B0 entries and frozen-core settings were **not verified from the full tables** in this survey, so no numerical a0/B0 target is invented. Fetch those tables and account for PAW versus NC, reference orbitals and zero-point corrections before acceptance.
- **Executable comparisons:** QE 7.6 pw.x can validate the KS input and EXX components on dev08. A working RPA total-energy workflow/executable in that installation is **not established**. If separately available, GPAW's documented Si RPA tutorial [L9], ABINIT or a licensed VASP workflow [L10] could compare response eigenvalues, frequency integrands, E_c and E(V); their availability is not assumed and none is installed by this task. Same-UPF NC comparisons are preferable; PAW/frozen-core mismatches require interpretation.

**Risks / estimate / order.** Slow basis/empty-state convergence, optical-limit errors, cancellation, inconsistent normalization, large response memory, and confusion between RPA, RPA+, SOSEX and QMC. RPA is not a general cure for strong static correlation in Mott oxides. **30–50 agent-days** for the explicit-response Si proof of concept excluding EXX; **20–35** for converged E(V), resource engineering and a reproducible material oracle. Order **9**: first a small trustworthy research tool, not 50-atom production RPA. Optimized response and metallic RPA are further tickets, not hidden inside this estimate.

## 8. cRPA: screened Hubbard U and J

**Established physics.** Define a target correlated subspace d and exclude **only screening transitions within it** [23,24]:

\[
\chi_r=\chi^0-\chi_d,\qquad W_r(\omega)=[1-v\chi_r(\omega)]^{-1}v,
\qquad W=[1-W_r\chi_d]^{-1}W_r.
\]

For localized orbitals w, retain a four-index interaction before reducing it:

\[
U_{ab,cd}(\omega)=\iint w_a^*(r)w_b(r)W_r(r,r',\omega)
w_c^*(r')w_d(r')\,dr\,dr'.
\]

Direct elements are `U_mm,nn`; exchange elements are `U_mn,nm`. State whether reported U is average intraorbital, average direct, or Slater F0; J is a specified off-diagonal exchange average. These conventions are not interchangeable. Static `omega=0` values can parameterize a low-energy model; dynamic U(omega) contains physics discarded in static +U. cRPA is not the linear-response constrained-DFT U method, and calculating U,J is not solving a Hubbard model.

**Missing machinery / implementation.** The engine lacks Wannier functions, overlaps between neighboring k points, disentanglement, orbital-window selection and a response engine. Isolated-band projector tests can start without full localization, but a credible oxide U needs a reproducible localized subspace. Build k-neighbor overlaps with reciprocal-boundary mapping, trial projections and band energies, then use a documented external Wannier interface rather than invent a general localization optimizer. Import unitary/disentanglement matrices with dimensions, phases/gauge checks and reconstruction diagnostics. cRPA extends the §7 response module with constrained transition weights/projections and a dielectric solve; implement both complete and residual response so the identity above is tested. Metals require validated intraband treatment (§2 plus a response extension). Simple subtraction by band index fails for entangled metal-d/oxygen-p bands; naive projected subtraction may violate causal/positive screening and needs a declared prescription [24].

**Libraries and licensing.** Wannier90 is the leading interface candidate [L11]. **Version matters:** upstream currently announces **v4.0.3 (2026-10-05), LGPL-2.1-or-later for v4.x**, whereas v3.1.0 and earlier were GPL v2-family. It is incorrect to call all current Wannier90 GPL-only. The mature community project supplies file and new library interfaces; a specific Apple Silicon Fortran/BLAS build and versioned overlap round trip have not been tested here. Recommend a **user-supplied standalone executable and file adapter** first, no source copying/vendoring/linking. Review the selected release and distribution model before use; LGPL linking is a new approval question, not covered by xcx's MPL approval. No permissive equally complete Wannier/disentanglement replacement was verified. Existing faer/rustfft cover projection and screening linear algebra on the engine side.

**Audit/state/coupling.** cRPA leaves KS density/occupations unchanged and outputs interactions, not a new additive total-energy correction. Do not add U as an energy or automatically rerun DFT+U and label it self-consistent cRPA. State includes target shells/sites, real-space orbitals or their reconstruction hashes, Wannier version, energy/frozen windows, gauge/local axes, spreads, projector metric, excluded screening definition, frequency/q/basis/empty-state settings and full U tensor with averages. +U consumes that U only if projector/subspace conventions match; otherwise an explicit mapping study is needed. Coupling exports a typed frequency-dependent interaction with provenance and ownership, not a scalar electrostatic potential. A DMFT embedding loop needs another double-counting and stationarity contract.

**Oracles.** Analytic limits: excluding nothing gives full W; excluding all response gives bare v; restoring chi_d reproduces W; Hermitian interaction tensors and invariant averages under the declared orbital transformations. Published oxide targets: Vaugier, Jiang and Biermann (2012) [23] report **SrVO3 t2g U=3.2 eV, J=0.44 eV in Table III**; its comparison prose instead quotes J=0.46 eV. Preserve this source discrepancy and resolve it before setting a strict J acceptance tolerance. Table III also gives **SrCrO3 U=2.7 eV, J=0.42 eV** and **SrMnO3 U=1.8 eV, J=0.39 eV** for the same t2g-model family. These are distinct from the more localized d–dp model. Miyake and Aryasetiawan's SrVO3 MLWF result **U=3.0 eV, J=0.43 eV** is quoted/discussed in [23]; treat it as secondary until the original settings are acquired. Published U is **subspace-dependent**, not an element constant. ABINIT has a documented SrVO3 cRPA tutorial [L12]; use only if a compatible installation becomes available. QE 7.6 on dev08 can check KS bands/projector populations and potentially export Wannier inputs after capability review; a cRPA oracle in dft5090 is not established. QE linear-response Hubbard U would validate a different definition, not certify cRPA.

**Dependencies / risk / estimate / order.** Needs spin/smearing for target metals, restart/orbitals, response (not necessarily the completed RPA trace-log energy), subspace infrastructure and subspace-matched reference data. Uncertain window choice, entanglement, semicore screening and NC core reconstruction can dominate differences. **15–25 agent-days** Wannier export/import and checks; **25–45** constrained response/tensor projection after §7 kernels; **15–25** oxide reproducibility and convergence. Order **10**; prefer an isolated-band toy case, then SrVO3, before entangled magnetic cathodes. A full first cRPA capability is a multi-milestone research program.

### Pseudopotential ecosystem decision

**Established/observed:** transition metals do **not** fundamentally require PAW or ultrasoft pseudopotentials. High-quality norm-conserving ONCV datasets exist; their semicore treatment, harder cutoffs and multiple projectors make them more demanding than the current Si fixture. Oxygen and transition-metal semicore states can determine practical cutoffs and oxidation-state transferability. PAW/USPP may reduce cost or improve access to reconstruction-sensitive observables; neither automatically validates redox energies.

- **PseudoDojo:** van Setten et al. (2018) [25], scalar/full-relativistic, standard/stringent and semicore choices. Upstream declares potential and input **data CC BY 4.0**, separately from the generator/framework code [L15]. Formats include UPF, psp8 and PSML; ONCVPSP natively emits UPF/psp8, with PSML produced through its documented additional path [L16]. UPF data is the initial route; no generator is linked or copied. Maintained web/repository distribution exists; the exact chosen release and hashes still need selection. Data is architecture independent, reader numerics must pass on Apple Silicon.
- **SG15 ONCV:** Schlipf and Gygi (2015) [26], distributed as NC UPF, with scalar and fully relativistic variants requiring separate compatibility checks. The dataset host timed out during this survey; **CC BY-SA 4.0** was verified in the Materials Cloud distributor's family-specific license manifest [L17], and UPF availability in ABACUS's dataset documentation [L18]. This is evidence for those distributed datasets, not blanket permission for every derivative. Pin archive/file provenance before use. Share-alike data licensing does not turn the engine source into GPL; keep potential redistribution separate and follow the repo's stricter **never commit UPF files** rule.

Recommendation: first audit one PseudoDojo Si/O/Fe/Mn/Ni set with recorded valence/semicore, NLCC, projector count, relativistic and XC properties. Extend the UPF2 reader and NLCC before claiming oxide support. Validate scattering/charge and equations of state against QE with identical files; assess magnetic moments and oxidation environments, not Si alone. Defer PAW/USPP until measured NC cost or a required observable justifies the much larger implementation.

## Recommended ordering and ticket boundaries

**Inference from the user's targets:** the shortest route to useful oxide/cathode science is reliable pseudopotentials, spin, occupations, +U and geometry derivatives. A long functional list on a Si-only parser is less useful than this connected set. Proposed stages (separate ownership/ADRs and review gates) are:

1. **Foundation, order 0:** ESLE phase B warm restart and immutable state records; audit dataset provenance; UPF2 dynamic ONCV projectors/atomic wavefunctions, NLCC. Preserve the scalar regression. Define spin/fractional-occupation and generalized energy ledgers jointly before implementation. No session/dashboard/README artifact outside this ticket is changed by this research task.
2. **Spin and metals, orders 1–2:** LSDA-PW92/spin PBE, optionally polarized PZ81; FD/Gaussian occupations, common-mu and fixed-M distinction; then MP/MV. Gate with zero-spin Si, Al and a magnetic metal using validated NC data. Disable unjustified symmetry reductions. Restart round trips include spin and f.
3. **Oxide electronic states, order 3:** validated atomic/Löwdin projectors, Dudarev energy/operator/audit, controlled starts and release of constraints in ESLE phase E. Validate NiO plus a cathode-relevant oxide and metastability/sensitivity. This does not wait for cRPA.
4. **Geometry and throughput, order 4:** ordinary NC/NLCC forces then enabled spin/+U derivatives; fixed-cell relaxation; stress and cutoff convergence then variable-cell optimization. Add symmetry only after full-grid comparisons, retaining an unreduced path and symmetry-breaking starts. Couple external drivers only through the existing coupling gates.
5. **Layered systems and functional improvements, orders 5–7:** gated periodic D3 first if a license-audited candidate works; otherwise expose the block. r2SCAN is an independent branch after generalized KS contracts. Plain EXX/PBE0, then approved HSE semilocal components, then ACE. Derivatives are explicit follow-up tickets before relaxing with each new functional. SCAN and D4 are conditional additions, not hidden dependencies of r2SCAN/HSE.
6. **Target branches, order 8:** finite-displacement phonons and basic MD after forces; constrained moments after spin/projectors; SOC after spinors/full-relativistic data, before anisotropy claims. Charged-defect physics, NEB, voltages and alloy workflows often outrank RPA in practical value. GPU work starts only from measured bottlenecks and an approved backend design; PAW/USPP requires a separate benefit case.
7. **Many-body research, orders 9–10:** empty-state/response representation and Si RPA proof of concept, full EXX+RPA convergence/E(V), then localized-orbital export/import and cRPA. Wannier adapter work can overlap response development, but oxide cRPA waits for metal response and subspace validation. Dynamic U, DMFT, GW/BSE and RPA derivatives remain later proposals.

Each implementation series should split into (a) model/units/contracts, (b) analytic kernel tests and energy derivative audit, (c) SCF integration and restart, (d) independent remote numerical fixture/convergence, (e) derivatives and performance as separate gates. No speed estimate in this document substitutes for profiling. A pilot through spin, smearing, ONCV/NLCC and +U is roughly **60–100 agent-days**, depending on overlap and dataset surprises; the additional forces/stress/geometry path is roughly **40–70**. These planning aggregates share infrastructure and must not be added blindly to every per-section range. RPA/cRPA are later multi-month-equivalent effort programs, not minor XC switches.

- **Initial material/data set:** recommend provenance-verified PseudoDojo NC Si/O/Fe/Mn/Ni, UPF2/ONCV and NLCC first; defer PAW/USPP until benchmarks show a need.
