TASK_ID: P8B-VOLUMETRIC
GOAL: Volumetric data output in formats that visualization programs read: Gaussian cube and XSF for the valence electron density, the electron localization function (ELF), the Hartree and total local potential, and the squared modulus of a selected band at a selected k point (partial charge). Read AGENTS.md, docs/cli.md, docs/physics-conventions.md (density, Hartree, FFT conventions), crates/dft-physics/src/density.rs, crates/dft-engine (ScfResult: density, orbitals per k, weights, occupations), crates/dft-cli/src/run.rs (how results are written).
OWNED_FILES: crates/dft-physics/src/volumetric.rs (NEW: kinetic-energy density and ELF from orbitals, potentials on the grid; plus a mod line in lib.rs), crates/dft-physics/tests/volumetric.rs (NEW), crates/dft-cli/src/volumetric_out.rs (NEW: cube and XSF writers, selection options), config additions in crates/dft-cli/src/config.rs (additive: optional `[output.volumetric]` table: which quantities, formats, band/k selection) and wiring in run.rs (additive; another ticket edits run.rs: keep to a few lines), crates/dft-cli/tests/ (new files), scripts/read_cube.py (NEW, standard library only: independent reader/validator used by the tests and by users), scripts/tests/test_read_cube.py (NEW), docs/howto-volumetric.md (NEW, for people who do not code: how to open the files in VESTA or other viewers, what each quantity means, the pseudopotential caveat), docs/cli.md (append), docs/decisions/0021-volumetric-output.md (NEW). Do not touch other files, manifests, Cargo.lock, tickets/, artifacts/, baselines, README. Another ticket (P8A) adds new CLI subcommands in parallel: keep your wiring local to run.rs and the new files.
DESIGN:
1. Quantities: (a) valence electron density n(r) in electrons/Bohr^3; (b) ELF = 1/(1 + chi^2) with chi = (tau - tau_W)/tau_h, where tau = (1/2) sum_k w_k sum_n f_n |grad psi_nk|^2 (the kinetic energy density from the orbitals, computed by FFT of i(k+G) c(G): state the gauge/phase convention: |grad psi|^2 is gauge independent), tau_W = |grad n|^2/(8 n) (von Weizsaecker) and tau_h = (3/10)(3 pi^2)^(2/3) n^(5/3) (uniform-gas value), unpolarized (spin-summed density n with occupation factor already included in f_n: be careful with the factor 2 and document it); regions with n below a threshold get ELF = 0 by a documented rule; the formula is valence-only: ELF from a norm-conserving pseudopotential calculation differs from an all-electron ELF in the core regions, state it in the how-to; (c) Hartree potential and the total local potential V_ion + V_H + V_xc (Hartree units, with the G = 0 convention stated); (d) |psi_nk(r)|^2 for a chosen band and k (and the partial charge summed over a band range) normalized so that its integral is 1 per band.
2. Formats: Gaussian cube (units Bohr, general non-orthogonal cell vectors with the voxel vectors a_i/N_i, origin (0,0,0), atoms with atomic number, the valence charge as the "charge" column, data in the standard fastest-index-last order; write the format exactly as readers expect and verify with your independent reader) and XSF (BEGIN_BLOCK_DATAGRID_3D with the general grid and the periodic-boundary convention: XSF grids repeat the first point, state how you handle it). Raw binary stays as it is today (density.bin). Atomic numbers: derive from the element symbol through a small documented table (IUPAC data, all 118 elements) in dft-geometry or the CLI (state where) or from an existing maintained permissive crate if you can justify it; no guesses for unknown labels (error).
3. Hot path rules: these outputs are produced once after the SCF; they may allocate; the orbitals needed for ELF must be taken from the final ScfResult (no recomputation of the SCF). With more than one k point, tau is accumulated with the k weights in a fixed order (deterministic for every thread count).
TESTS: (a) analytic ELF checks: a uniform density gives ELF = 1/2 exactly (chi = 1); a single real normalized Gaussian-like orbital in a large cell (one occupied band with occupation 2 spin-summed, or a one-electron fixture) gives tau = tau_W and ELF = 1 where n is above the threshold (state the fixture and tolerance 1e-10); (b) tau of a plane wave: tau = (1/2)|k+G|^2 n for one plane wave; (c) cube round trip: write then read with scripts/read_cube.py, compare grid values bitwise to the written decimal precision (state precision 1e-12 relative), the cell vectors (skew cell, left-handed cell), the atom list and the integral of the density equal to N electrons within 1e-10; (d) XSF structure test; (e) |psi|^2 normalization per band; (f) results identical for threads 1 and 4; (g) scripts/check.sh and python3 -I -m unittest discover -s scripts/tests green. A real-Si example run (ignored test with an environment variable for the UPF path, never committed) writes the files and reports the density integral and the min/max of ELF.
ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA: all tests pass; files open in a standard reader (verified by the independent Python reader); documented conventions and caveats; no new dependency (or one with license, maintenance and Apple Silicon justification in docs/dependencies.md, requiring Claude's approval: prefer none); no unsafe.
NON_GOALS: spin densities, all-electron reconstruction, isosurface rendering (the user's viewer does it), performance work.
GIT: Leave changes uncommitted and say so.
