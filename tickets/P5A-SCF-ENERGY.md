DESIGN REQUIREMENTS (record API and reasons in docs/decisions/0009-scf-and-energy.md):
1. Mixers in dft-solvers: a `DensityMixer` trait over real-space density arrays (state precisely what is mixed, the input/output pair semantics, and the residual definition R = n_out - n_in), `LinearMixer` (parameter beta) and `PulayMixer` (Pulay/DIIS in the Anderson form, history length m with default 6, a stated regularization for ill-conditioned history, history reset rule, deterministic, small dense solves through dft-numerics only). Mixers preserve total charge: after mixing, the mean density corresponds to exactly N electrons within roundoff (state how).
2. SCF driver in dft-engine. Input: Cell, atoms with species and pseudopotentials, Ecut (Hartree), number of electrons from z_valence, number of bands (default occupied + extra, extra default 4), occupations (spin-unpolarized insulator: f = 2 for the lowest N_el/2 bands, 0 for others; reject odd electron counts), LDA-PZ, mixing choice and parameters, convergence tolerances, eigensolver tolerance schedule, seed. Start from a uniform density n = N_el/Omega. Each iteration: V_eff = V_ion(r) + V_H[n_in](r) + V_xc[n_in](r) on the FFT grid (ionic local part computed once); Hamiltonian apply plan reuse; Davidson with the diagonal preconditioner from the kinetic energy, warm-started with the previous iteration's eigenvectors; density from the lowest occupied bands; residual; mixing.
3. Convergence is declared ONLY when all hold on the same iteration: (a) density residual L2 norm sqrt(integral (n_out - n_in)^2 d^3r)/N_el <= density_tolerance, (b) |E_k - E_{k-1}| <= energy_tolerance, (c) maximum eigen residual of the occupied bands <= eigen_tolerance (final iteration solved to a tolerance at least as tight as eigen_tolerance), (d) |integral n d^3r - N_el| / N_el <= 1e-8. Energy change alone never converges the SCF. Defaults: density 1e-7, energy 1e-8 Ha, eigen 1e-8 Ha. Non-convergence within max_iterations returns a typed error carrying the history; divergence (non-finite values, or the residual growing beyond a stated factor of its best value for a stated number of iterations) returns a distinct typed error. No logging, JSON or file I/O in the numerical loop; per-iteration records (energy, dE, density residual, max eigen residual, Davidson iterations, charge error, phase timings) are collected in memory and returned.
4. Total energy (Hartree): E = T + E_loc + E_NL + E_H + E_xc + E_ewald, each term computed from the final orbitals and their density n_out: T = sum f 0.5*|G|^2|c|^2; E_NL = sum f <psi|V_NL|psi>; E_loc = Omega * sum_G conj(n(G)) V_ion(G) (including the G=0 alpha-Z term, state how it enters: Omega * n(0) * V_ion(0) = N_el * (sum over atoms of alpha-Z contributions)); E_H and E_xc from P3A; E_ewald from P3A with the point-charge convention; also report the double-counting integral and the band-energy form E_band - integral V_H n_out... (state the exact identity). Return a struct with every term, the eigenvalues, occupations, the converged density, charge, iteration history and timings, and expose total energy in Ha and Ry. Provide both (i) the direct functional above evaluated at n_out and (ii) the Harris-Foulkes-type functional evaluated at n_in using the eigenvalue sum; at convergence they must agree within a derived bound and the difference must decrease quadratically-ish with the residual (test the trend).
5. Independent electrostatic cross-check (REQUIRED, this is the key gate for the G=0 and Ewald conventions). Use a synthetic ion type with local potential V_I(r) = -Z_I*erf(r/rc)/r exactly (no nonlocal part), i.e. a Gaussian ionic charge rho_I(r) = Z_I*(pi*rc^2)^(-3/2)*exp(-r^2/rc^2), and a prescribed smooth positive periodic electron density n(r) built from Gaussians with sum_G n(0)*Omega = sum Z (a neutral cell). The direct electrostatic energy of the neutral system of Gaussian ions plus electrons is E_direct = 0.5 * Omega * sum_{G != 0} 4*pi*|rho_ion(G) - n(G)|^2/|G|^2 (pure reciprocal-space formula, no alpha-Z, no Ewald). Your engine path computes E_H[n] + E_loc[n] (including the G=0 alpha-Z term from the P2A local transform) + E_ewald(point charges). Derive analytically the exact constant C that the point-charge Ewald convention differs from the smeared-ion interaction energy (the self-energy of the Gaussian ionic charges plus the negligible pair-smearing corrections that vanish when pairs are much farther apart than rc; derive C, state it, and state its size) and verify E_engine + C = E_direct to 1e-9 relative for: at least three rc values, two different cells (fcc primitive with two atoms; a skewed triclinic cell with two or three atoms of different Z), two or more different density shapes, and a rigid translation of all atoms. A failure here means the alpha-Z bookkeeping or the Hartree/Ewald G=0 convention is wrong: investigate and fix, never loosen.
